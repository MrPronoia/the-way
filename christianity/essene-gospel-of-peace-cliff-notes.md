# The Essene Gospel of Peace (Book 1) — Cliff Notes & Quick Reference

Quick-reference breakdown of Jesus's teachings on natural living, the Earthly Mother, and healing through nature's angels. For the full text, see `Incoming/essene-gospel-of-peace-book-1-full-text.md`.

---

## One-Sentence Summary

Jesus teaches that sickness comes from violating the laws of the Earthly Mother (nature), and healing comes from returning to her through fasting, communion with her angels (air, water, sunlight, earth), vegetarianism, and living food — presenting a **nature-based, body-honoring spirituality** completely absent from the canonical gospels.

---

## Why This Text Matters

This is not the Jesus of Paul's letters or the Nicene Creed. This Jesus:
- **Names a Feminine Divine** — the "Earthly Mother" as co-equal with the Heavenly Father
- **Teaches vegetarianism** as a divine commandment, not a dietary preference
- **Treats disease as spiritual consequence** — sickness from violating natural law, not demonic possession
- **Prescribes specific physical practices** — water fasting, sun exposure, fresh air, enemas
- **Presents the body as sacred** — "the temple of the spirit," to be honored, not mortified

Whether authentically Essene or a 20th-century composition, the text captures a stream of early Jewish-Christian practice that was systematically excluded from canon. It fills the gap between Jesus the healer and Jesus the theologian — and suggests his healing methods were practical, not miraculous.

> **Note on provenance:** Scholarly consensus considers this text a modern composition by Szekely rather than an ancient manuscript. No original has been independently verified. The teachings are included here for their content, not as authenticated ancient scripture.

## The Two Parents — The Core Framework
*(Book 1, the Earthly Mother and Heavenly Father teaching)*

**Everything in this text flows from one teaching: you have two divine parents.**

| Parent | Domain | What They Give | Your Relationship |
|--------|--------|----------------|-------------------|
| **Heavenly Father** | Spirit, eternal life, truth, holy spirit | Your spirit, wisdom, eternal life | "His love gives to all life everlasting" |
| **Earthly Mother** | Body, physical life, nature, healing | Your blood, bones, flesh, breath, senses | "She bore you, she gives you life" |

> "Honor your Earthly Mother and keep all her laws, that your days may be long on this earth, and honor your Heavenly Father that Eternal Life may be yours in the heavens."

**The radical claim:** You cannot reach the Heavenly Father without first honoring the Earthly Mother. The body is not a prison — it's a prerequisite.

> "No one can reach the Heavenly Father unless through the Earthly Mother. Even as no newborn babe can understand the teaching of his father till his mother has suckled him, bathed him, nursed him, put him to sleep and nurtured him."

**This inverts the standard Christian hierarchy that treats spirit over body, heaven over earth, Father over Mother.**

## The Angels of the Earthly Mother — The Four Healing Forces
*(Book 1, the Angels of the Earthly Mother teaching)*

Jesus names four "angels" — forces of nature that heal and purify. These aren't winged beings; they're the living powers of air, water, sunlight, and earth.

### Angel of Air *(Book 1)*
**Practice:** Go to forests and fields. Remove clothing. Breathe deeply.
> "Put off your shoes and your clothing and suffer the angel of air to embrace all your body. Then breathe long and deeply, that the angel of air may be brought within you."

**What it does:** "The angel of air shall cast out of your body all uncleannesses which defiled it without and within."

**The teaching:** "All must be born again by air and by truth" — a reframing of John 3:3 (born of water and spirit) to include air/breath.

### Angel of Water *(Book 1)*
**Practice:** Immerse in rivers/streams. Move the body in water. Also: internal cleansing (enemas).
> "Cast yourselves wholly into his enfolding arms, and as often as you move the air with your breath, move with your body the water also."

**The internal baptism:**
> "Seek a large trailing gourd... fill it with water from the river which the sun has warmed... suffer the end of the stalk to enter your hinder parts, that the water may flow through all your bowels."

**What it does:** "The angel of water shall cast out of your body all uncleannesses which defiled it without and within."

**This is the most controversial passage — Jesus explicitly teaching colon cleansing as spiritual practice. Whether authentic or not, it represents a view of physical purification as prerequisite to spiritual purification.**

### Angel of Sunlight *(Book 1)*
**Practice:** Expose the body to direct sunlight.
> "Put off your shoes and your clothing and suffer the angel of sunlight to embrace all your body. Then breathe long and deeply, that the angel of sunlight may be brought within you."

**What it does:** "The angel of sunlight shall cast out of your body all evil-smelling and unclean things... even as the darkness of night fades before the brightness of the rising sun."

**The teaching:** "All must be born again of sun and of truth" — another expansion of the born-again teaching to include natural elements.

### Angel of Earth *(Book 1)*
**Practice:** Sink feet into clay/mud near warm water.
> "Sink your feet in the mire, that the embrace of the angel of earth may draw out from your bones all uncleanness and all disease."

**What it heals:** Bone diseases, joint problems, arthritis ("the knots of your bones will vanish away").

## The Body as Mirror — What Sickness Reveals
*(Book 1, the body-earth correspondence teaching)*

**The teaching:** Your physical state reflects your spiritual state. Each part of your body corresponds to the Earthly Mother's body. When you violate her laws, those parts decay.

| Your Body | Earthly Mother's Body | What Happens When You Sin |
|-----------|----------------------|---------------------------|
| Blood | "Falls from clouds, leaps from earth, babbles in brooks" | "Becomes thick and evil-smelling... clots and blackens, like the night of death" |
| Breath | "Azure in heights of heavens, soughs in mountain tops" | "Becomes short and stifled, full of pain and evil-smelling" |
| Bones | "Rocks and stones... giants that lie sleeping" | "Becomes hard and knotted... melts away within and breaks asunder" |
| Flesh | "Waxes yellow and red in the fruits of the trees" | "Waxes fat and watery; it rots and putrefies, with scabs and boils" |
| Eyes | "Colors of the Earthly Mother" | "Grow dim, till dark night enshrouds them" |
| Ears | "Sounds of the Earthly Mother" | "Become stopped, like the silence of the grave" |
| Bowels | "Invisible depths of the earth" | "Become full with abominable filthiness... multitudes of abominable worms" |

**The principle:** "Your bodies become what your foods are, even as your spirits, likewise, become what your thoughts are."

## The Satan Teaching — Disease as Demonic Debt
*(Book 1, the Satan and disease teaching)*

**Satan in this text is not a cosmic rebel but a principle of decay that operates through lifestyle violation.**

> "Beelzebub, the prince of all devils, the source of every evil, lies in wait in the body of all the Sons of Men. He is death, the lord of every plague."

**How Satan operates:** Through temptation to excess — riches, power, gluttony, wine, fornication, slothfulness.

> "In the day that the Sons of Men have already become the slaves of all these vanities and abominations, then in payment thereof he snatches from the Sons of Men all those things which the Earthly Mother gave them so abundantly."

**The Prodigal Son parable reframed:** Satan is the creditor. Your sins are debts. Your diseases are payment. Fasting cancels the debt.

> "The wicked creditor is Satan. The debts are diseases. The heavy labor is pains. The prodigal son, he is yourselves."

**The math:** Seven years of sin = seven days of fasting to clear. "Seven times seven years" = seven times seven days.

## The Vegetarian Commandment — The Most Radical Teaching
*(Book 1, the vegetarian commandment and original divine law)*

**This is the passage that changed everything. Jesus frames vegetarianism as original divine law, with Moses's allowance of meat as a concession to human weakness.**

### The Original Command (Genesis 1:29 expanded)
> "Behold, I have given you every herb bearing seed, which is upon the face of all the earth, and every tree, in the which is the fruit of a tree yielding seed; to you it shall be for meat. And to every beast of the earth, and to every fowl of the air, and to everything that creepeth upon the earth, wherein there is breath of life, I give every green herb for meat. Also the milk of every thing that moveth and liveth upon earth shall be meat for you... But flesh, and the blood which quickens it, shall ye not eat."

### The Law of Karmic Return
> "He who kills, kills himself, and whoso eats the flesh of slain beasts, eats of the body of death. For in his blood every drop of their blood turns to poison; in his breath their breath to stink; in his flesh their flesh to boils; in his bones their bones to chalk; in his bowels their bowels to decay."

**The principle:** "Eye for eye, tooth for tooth... life for life, death for death. For the wages of sin is death."

### Why Moses Allowed Meat
> "Moses received not his laws from God in writing, but through the living word... God gave, by Moses, ten commandments to your forefathers. 'These commandments are hard,' said your forefathers, and they could not keep them. When Moses saw this, he had compassion on his people, and would not that they perish... Suffer, Lord, that I give them other laws, that they may not perish."

**The claim:** The ten commandments became "ten times ten" (100), then the Pharisees made "a hundred times ten" (1,000). But "the more nigh are the commandments to God, the less do we need."

| Level | Number of Laws | Source |
|-------|---------------|--------|
| Pharisees/Scribes | "Innumerable" | Human accumulation |
| Moses | 100 | Compassion for human weakness |
| Son of Man | 7 | What humans can actually follow |
| Angels | 3 | (Air, water, sunlight?) |
| God | 1 | (Love? Unity?) |

### The Exception — Self-Defense
> "If I see a wild beast rend my brother in the forest? Shall I let my brother perish, or kill the wild beast?"

> "You do not transgress the law if you kill the wild beast to save your brother's life... But he who kills the beast without a cause, though the beast attack him not, through lust for slaughter, or for its flesh, or for its hide, or yet for its tusks, evil is the deed which he does."

## Living Food vs. Dead Food — The Cooking Prohibition
*(Book 1, the fire of life vs. fire of death teaching)*

**Another radical teaching: cooked food = dead food = death in the body.**

> "Kill neither men, nor beasts, nor yet the food which goes into your mouth. For if you eat living food, the same will quicken you, but if you kill your food, the dead food will kill you also. For life comes only from life, and from death comes always death."

### The Fire Teaching
| Fire | Source | Effect |
|------|--------|--------|
| **Fire of Life** | "In you, in your blood, and in your bodies" | Quickens, heals |
| **Fire of Death** | "The fire which blazes outside your body, which is hotter than your blood" | Kills food, kills bodies |

> "Eat nothing, therefore, which a stronger fire than the fire of life has killed."

### The Sun-Baked Bread
**Jesus teaches how to make "bread" without fire:**
1. Moisten wheat with water ("that the angel of water may enter it")
2. Set it in the air ("that the angel of air also may embrace it")
3. Leave it in the sun from morning to evening
4. Crush the sprouted grain
5. Make thin wafers
6. Dry in the sun, turning at noon

> "The same sun which, with the fire of life, made the wheat to grow and ripen, must cook your bread with the same fire. For the fire of the sun gives life to the wheat, to the bread, and to the body. But the fire of death kills the wheat, the bread, and the body."

**This is essentially a recipe for Essene bread / sprouted grain wafers — still made today by raw food advocates.**

## The Eating Laws — Practical Instructions
*(Book 1, dietary laws and seasonal eating)*

### What to Eat
- Fruits of trees
- Grasses of fields
- Milk of beasts
- Honey of bees
- "Everything beyond these is of Satan"

### How to Eat
| Rule | Teaching |
|------|----------|
| **Frequency** | "He who eats more than twice in the day does in him the work of Satan" — ideally once daily "when the sun is highest" |
| **Quantity** | "When your body is sated... always eat less by a third" |
| **Variety** | "Be content with two or three sorts of food... do not devour all things" |
| **Mixing** | "Cook not, neither mix all things one with another, lest your bowels become as steaming bogs" |
| **Breathing** | "Have above you the angel of air... breathe long and deeply at all your meals" |
| **Chewing** | "Chew well your food with your teeth, that it become water" |
| **Speed** | "Eat slowly, as it were a prayer you make to the Lord" |
| **Mood** | "Put naught upon the altar of the Lord when your spirit is vexed, neither think upon any one with anger in the temple of God" |

### The Seasonal Calendar
| Month | Food |
|-------|------|
| Ijar | Barley |
| Sivan | Wheat ("the most perfect among all seed-bearing herbs") |
| Tammuz | Sour grape |
| Elul | Grape juice as drink |
| Marchesvan | Dried sweet grape with almonds |
| Ab, Shebat | Figs |
| Thebet | Herbs after rain + milk |

### The Sabbath Fast
> "Every seventh day is holy and consecrated to God. On six days feed your body with the gifts of the Earthly Mother, but on the seventh day sanctify your body for your Heavenly Father. On the seventh day eat not any earthly food, but live only on the words of God."

## The Prayers — Father and Mother
*(Book 1, the two prayers)*

**The Lord's Prayer appears, followed by a companion prayer to the Earthly Mother — unique to this text.**

### To the Heavenly Father
> "Our Father which art in heaven, hallowed be thy name. Thy kingdom come. Thy will be done on earth as it is in heaven. Give us this day our daily bread. And forgive us our debts, as we forgive our debtors. And lead us not into temptation, but deliver us from evil. For thine is the kingdom, the power, and the glory, for ever. Amen."

### To the Earthly Mother
> "Our Mother which art upon earth, hallowed be thy name. Thy kingdom come, and thy will be done in us, as it is in thee. As thou sendest every day thy angels, send them to us also. Forgive us our sins, as we atone all our sins against thee. And lead us not into sickness, but deliver us from all evil, for thine is the earth, the body, and the health. Amen."

**The parallel structure:**
| Father | Mother |
|--------|--------|
| In heaven | Upon earth |
| Thy will done on earth | Thy will done in us |
| Give us daily bread | Send us thy angels |
| Forgive debts as we forgive | Forgive sins as we atone |
| Lead not into temptation | Lead not into sickness |
| Deliver from evil | Deliver from evil |
| Kingdom, power, glory | Earth, body, health |

## The Scripture Teaching — Living Word vs. Dead Text
*(Book 1, the living word teaching)*

**One of the most provocative passages: Jesus criticizing reliance on scripture.**

> "Seek not the law in your scriptures, for the law is life, whereas the scripture is dead. I tell you truly, Moses received not his laws from God in writing, but through the living word. The law is living word of living God to living prophets for living men."

**Where to find God's law:**
> "God wrote not the laws in the pages of books, but in your heart and in your spirit. They are in your breath, your blood, your bone; in your flesh, your bowels, your eyes, your ears, and in every little part of your body. They are present in the air, in the water, in the earth, in the plants, in the sunbeams, in the depths and in the heights."

**The critique:**
> "I tell you truly, that the scripture is the work of man, but life and all its hosts are the work of our God. Wherefore do you not listen to the words of God which are written in His works? And wherefore do you study the dead scriptures which are the work of the hands of men?"

**This is exactly what you would expect from an Essene text — priority of direct experience over institutional interpretation.**

## The Healing Protocol — Summary
*(Synthesized from Book 1)*

**The complete program Jesus prescribes:**

### Phase 1: External Purification
1. **Air** — Go to nature, strip, breathe deeply
2. **Water** — Immerse in running water, move the body
3. **Sunlight** — Expose body to direct sun
4. **Earth** — Sink feet in warm mud/clay

### Phase 2: Internal Purification
1. **Water fasting** — Duration based on severity of "debt"
2. **Internal baptism** — Daily water enemas until discharge runs clear
3. **Breath work** — Continuous deep breathing

### Phase 3: Dietary Reset
1. **Living food only** — No cooked food, no meat
2. **Simple combinations** — Two or three foods per meal
3. **Proper timing** — Once or twice daily, at solar noon and/or sunset
4. **Proper manner** — Slow, chewing to liquid, breathing, gratitude, no anger

### Phase 4: Ongoing Practice
1. **Weekly sabbath fast** — No earthly food on seventh day
2. **Seasonal eating** — Follow the calendar
3. **Daily communion** — With the angels (nature) and both divine parents
4. **Sin no more** — Don't rebuild the debt

## How to Read This Text

**If you're coming from canonical Christianity:**
This will feel heretical at first. The Earthly Mother, vegetarianism, enemas — none of this is in your Bible. But notice: the tone is still Jesus. The parables work. The Lord's Prayer is intact. Consider this as one stream of early Jesus teaching that didn't make it through the canonical filter.

**If you're interested in the Essene connection:**
This is the strongest single text linking Jesus to Essene practice. The Dead Sea Scrolls show Essenes emphasized purity, communal meals, water rituals, and separation from Temple corruption. This text fills in the practical details.

**If you're interested in alternative health:**
This reads like an ancient naturopathic manual. Fasting, sunlight, fresh air, vegetarianism, food combining, mindful eating, colon cleansing — all of it is here, attributed to Jesus. Whether authentic or not, the practices are coherent with natural healing traditions worldwide.

**If you're tracking the Feminine Divine:**
The "Earthly Mother" passages are gold. This is one of the clearest expressions of a feminine divine principle in the Jesus tradition, complete with a parallel prayer. Compare to Shekinah, Sophia, Shakti.

**On authenticity:**
Scholarly consensus doubts Szekely's claimed sources (Vatican Aramaic and Habsburg Slavonic manuscripts). The text may be a 20th-century composition drawing on genuine Essene themes. This doesn't mean it's worthless — it means read it for what it teaches, not what it proves historically.

## Key Passages for Memorization

**The Two Parents:**
> "I tell you truly, you are one with the Earthly Mother; she is in you, and you in her. Of her were you born, in her do you live, and to her shall you return again."

**The Body-Earth Identity:**
> "For your breath is her breath; your blood her blood; your bone her bone; your flesh her flesh; your bowels her bowels; your eyes and your ears are her eyes and her ears."

**The Vegetarian Law:**
> "Kill not, neither eat the flesh of your innocent prey, lest you become the slaves of Satan. For that is the path of sufferings, and it leads unto death."

**Living vs. Dead Food:**
> "If you eat living food, the same will quicken you, but if you kill your food, the dead food will kill you also. For life comes only from life, and from death comes always death."

**Scripture vs. Nature:**
> "Seek not the law in your scriptures, for the law is life, whereas the scripture is dead... God wrote not the laws in the pages of books, but in your heart and in your spirit."

**The Great Declaration:**
> "FOR YOUR HEAVENLY FATHER IS LOVE. FOR YOUR EARTHLY MOTHER IS LOVE. FOR THE SON OF MAN IS LOVE."

**The Path:**
> "Follow, therefore, first, only the laws of your Earthly Mother... And when her angels shall have cleansed and renewed your bodies and strengthened your eyes, you will be able to bear the light of our Heavenly Father."
