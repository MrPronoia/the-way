# What Jesus Actually Said

## The Original Message

What if the most influential teacher in history has been misunderstood for 2,000 years? In red-letter Bibles, Jesus's own words are printed in red. Read just the red. Then read what the church teaches. They don't match.

---

## Three Questions Every Christian Should Ask

His own words. Look them up.

### Where is God?

**Jesus:** "The kingdom of God is **within you**." — Luke 17:21

**The church:** In heaven. Above you. Accessed through a priest, a building, a ritual.

### Where is the Kingdom?

**Jesus:** "The Father's kingdom is spread out upon the earth, and **people don't see it**." — Gospel of Thomas, Saying 113

**The church:** Later. After you die. Or after the rapture — which was invented in the 1830s by a man recovering from a horse injury.

### How are you saved?

**Jesus:** "If you want to enter eternal life, **keep the commandments**." — Matthew 19:17

**The church:** Confess Jesus as Lord, believe he died for your sins, accept the blood sacrifice.

Jesus said keep the commandments. Paul — who never met him — said confess and believe. The church followed Paul.

## Three Discoveries That Changed Everything

**The Dead Sea Scrolls** (1947) — 950 manuscripts hidden in caves for 2,000 years. They reveal the Essene community Jesus emerged from: shared vocabulary, shared practices, shared theology. Anti-sacrifice. Prayer over blood. A council of twelve.

**The Gospel of Thomas** (1945) — 114 sayings of Jesus with no crucifixion narrative, no resurrection theology, no Pauline framework. Just his words: the Kingdom within, self-knowledge as salvation, God in all things. Many scholars date its earliest layer to 50–70 CE — potentially before any canonical gospel.

**The Ethiopian Bible** — 81 books. 15 more than the Western canon. Preserved since the 4th century, never filtered through Nicaea or Rome. Paul's 13 letters are there — but they're 13 out of 35 New Testament books, not 13 out of 27. The additional texts all emphasize what Paul de-emphasized: commandments, works, and the authority of the Twelve who actually knew Jesus.

## What They Don't Teach You

**The rapture was invented in the 1830s.** One man — John Nelson Darby — created it while recovering from a horse injury. For 1,800 years, no Christian had ever heard of it. Not the early church, not Augustine, not Luther, not Calvin.

**Isaac Newton proved two key Trinity verses were forged.** 1 Timothy 3:16 was physically altered — one pen stroke changed "who" to "God." 1 John 5:7-8 was inserted centuries after the original text. The greatest scientific mind in Western history had to keep this secret because anti-Trinitarianism was illegal.

**For 300 years, every Christian was a pacifist.** Every major church father said the same thing: Christians cannot kill. Then Constantine conquered under the cross in 312 CE, and within a century, only Christians could serve in the Roman military.

**Jesus, his brother James, and all twelve apostles were documented vegetarians.** James the Just was vegetarian from birth — same household as Jesus. Eusebius attested all twelve apostles abstained from meat. No early source documents any of them eating it. Jesus said "I desire mercy, not sacrifice" — and he meant it for all living things.
