# What Jesus Actually Said

## The Original Message

What if the most influential teacher in history has been misunderstood for 2,000 years? In red-letter Bibles, Jesus's own words are printed in red. Read just the red. Then read what the church teaches. They don't match.

## Three Questions Every Christian Should Ask

His own words. Look them up.

### Where is God?

**Jesus:** "The kingdom of God is **within you**." — [Luke 17:21](../../docs/jesus/what-jesus-taught.md#the-kingdom-is-within-you--now)

**The church:** In heaven. Above you. Accessed through a priest, a building, a ritual.

### Where is the Kingdom?

**Jesus:** "The Father's kingdom is spread out upon the earth, and **people don't see it**." — [Gospel of Thomas, Saying 113](../../docs/jesus/the-evidence.md#the-gospel-of-thomas)

**The church:** Later. After you die. Or after the [rapture](../../docs/jesus/the-rapture.md) — which was [invented in the 1830s](../../docs/jesus/the-rapture.md#john-nelson-darby--the-man-who-changed-everything) by a man recovering from a horse injury.

### How are you saved?

**Jesus:** "If you want to enter eternal life, **keep the commandments**." — [Matthew 19:17](../../docs/jesus/what-jesus-taught.md#keep-the-commandments)

**The church:** Confess Jesus as Lord, believe he died for your sins, accept the blood sacrifice.

Jesus said keep the commandments. [Paul](../../docs/jesus/the-paul-problem.md) — who never met him — said confess and believe. The church followed Paul.

## The Original Message Was Simpler

- **The Kingdom of God is within you** — now, not after death
- **Forgive and be forgiven** — no blood required
- **Keep the commandments** — love God, love your neighbor
- **Know yourself** — "[become what he was](christconsciousnessvision.md)," not just a worshipper of what he was
- **Direct experience over institutional religion** — the Kingdom is already here
- **"I desire mercy, not sacrifice"** — he said it [twice](../../docs/jesus/thesis.md)

Not a new religion. The original one — before it was rewritten.

## Three Discoveries That Changed Everything

**[The Dead Sea Scrolls](dead-sea-scrolls/00-overview.md)** (1947) — 950 manuscripts hidden in caves for 2,000 years. They reveal the [Essene community](2026-02-25-essene-nazarene-ebionite-lineage.md) Jesus emerged from: shared vocabulary, shared practices, shared theology. [Anti-sacrifice. Prayer over blood. A council of twelve.](dead-sea-scrolls/cliff-notes-quick-reference.md)

**[The Gospel of Thomas](Incoming/gospel-of-thomas-cliff-notes.md)** (1945) — 114 sayings of Jesus with no crucifixion narrative, no resurrection theology, no Pauline framework. Just his words: the Kingdom within, self-knowledge as salvation, God in all things. Many scholars date its earliest layer to 50–70 CE — potentially before any canonical gospel.

**[The Ethiopian Bible](ethiopian-bible/00-overview.md)** — 81 books. 15 more than the Western canon. Preserved since the 4th century, never filtered through Nicaea or Rome. Paul's 13 letters are there — but they're 13 out of 35 New Testament books, not 13 out of 27. The additional texts all emphasize what Paul de-emphasized: [commandments, works, and the authority of the Twelve](ethiopian-bible/cliff-notes-quick-reference.md) who actually knew Jesus.

## What They Don't Teach You

**The rapture was [invented in the 1830s](../../docs/jesus/the-rapture.md#john-nelson-darby--the-man-who-changed-everything).** One man — John Nelson Darby — created it while recovering from a horse injury. For [1,800 years](../../docs/jesus/the-rapture.md#1800-years-of-christians-who-never-heard-of-it), no Christian had ever heard of it. Not the early church, not Augustine, not Luther, not Calvin.

**Isaac Newton [proved two key Trinity verses were forged](trinity-fourth-century-construction.md).** 1 Timothy 3:16 was physically altered — one pen stroke changed "who" to "God." 1 John 5:7-8 was inserted centuries after the original text. The greatest scientific mind in Western history had to keep this secret because anti-Trinitarianism was illegal.

**For [300 years, every Christian was a pacifist](early-christian-pacifism-before-constantine.md).** Every major church father said the same thing: Christians cannot kill. Then Constantine conquered under the cross in 312 CE, and within a century, only Christians could serve in the Roman military.

**Jesus, his brother James, and [all twelve apostles were documented vegetarians](biblical-case-for-vegetarian-jesus.md).** James the Just was vegetarian from birth — same household as Jesus. Eusebius attested all twelve apostles abstained from meat. No early source documents any of them eating it. Jesus said "I desire mercy, not sacrifice" — and he meant it for all living things.
