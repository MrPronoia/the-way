# The Practice

**What you do with the Christ Consciousness Vision. Daily. Specific. Based on what Jesus actually said.**

The Vision tells you how a Christ-Conscious being sees the world. This tells you how to start seeing it yourself. Not someday. Today.

---

## Morning — Drink From His Mouth

> *"Whoever drinks from my mouth will become like me."*
> — Gospel of Thomas, Saying 108

Pick one saying from the Gospel of Thomas. Don't analyze it. Don't look up what scholars say about it. Read it once. Sit with it. Let it sit with you throughout the day.

The sayings are designed to work on you below the level of understanding. Saying 1: *"Whoever discovers the interpretation of these sayings will not taste death."* The interpretation isn't intellectual. It's experiential. You don't figure it out — you become it.

**Start with these five. One per day. Repeat the cycle.**

1. *"The Father's kingdom is spread out upon the earth, and people don't see it."* (113)
2. *"If you bring forth what is within you, what you have will save you."* (70)
3. *"There is light within a person of light, and it lights up the whole world."* (24)
4. *"Be passersby."* (42)
5. *"When you make the two into one, and when you make the inner like the outer and the outer like the inner... then you will enter the kingdom."* (22)

## Throughout the Day — Christ Vision

> *"Whatever you did for the least of these, you did for me."*
> — Matthew 25:40

> *"Split a piece of wood; I am there. Lift up a stone, and you will find me there."*
> — Gospel of Thomas, Saying 77

Look at every person you encounter and see the divine in them. Not metaphorically. Practice it. The cashier. The driver who cuts you off. The person you disagree with. The one you love. The one who annoys you.

Every tradition confirms this: God is not somewhere else. God is looking out from behind every pair of eyes you meet. The Kabbalists call them divine sparks. The Sufis call it the Unity of Being. Ra states: *"You are every thing, every being, every emotion, every event, every situation. You are unity. You are infinity"* (Ra Material, Session 1, *The Law of One*). Jesus said it plainest: *"Whatever you did for the least of these, you did for me."*

**The practical move:** When you see someone — anyone — silently bless them. You don't need words. You don't need them to know. Just hold, for one second, the awareness that what's looking out of their eyes is the same thing looking out of yours.

Do this with strangers first. It's easier. Then try it with the people who trigger you.

## When Triggered — Forgive Immediately

> *"Father, forgive them, for they know not what they do."*
> — Luke 23:34

> *"Forgive and you will be forgiven."*
> — Luke 6:37

When someone wrongs you, when anger flares, when you want to judge — this is the practice. Not later. Not after you've processed it. Now.

The Christ-Conscious response is not "they're right" or "it doesn't matter." It's: *they don't know what they're doing.* They're asleep. They're acting from pain, from conditioning, from unconsciousness. Seeing that clearly is not weakness — it's the clearest possible seeing.

Unforgiveness is you drinking poison. The other person doesn't feel it. You do. Every tradition teaches this. Ra teaches that *"forgiveness of other-self is forgiveness of self"* (Ra Material, Session 18, *The Law of One*) — and identifies Jesus's forgiveness on the cross as the act that absolved his karma entirely (Ra Material, Session 17). Jesus demonstrated it at the most extreme possible moment — on the cross, under torture, with his killers watching.

**The practical move:** When triggered, before you react, say internally: *"They know not what they do."* Not as a platitude. As a diagnosis. They are asleep. You don't punish someone for being asleep. You wait for them to wake up.

## Speech — Say Less. Mean It.

> *"Let your yes be yes and your no be no."*
> — Matthew 5:37

> *"If you fast, you will bring sin upon yourselves, and if you pray, you will be condemned."*
> — Gospel of Thomas, Saying 14

Jesus was against empty performance — in speech, in ritual, in religion. Saying 14 is devastating: fasting and praying done without inner reality are worse than doing nothing. They create the illusion of spirituality while the inside stays untouched.

The same applies to speech. Most words are noise. Most conversations are performances. Most of what we say is designed to manage how others perceive us, not to communicate truth.

**The practical move:** Before speaking, run it through one filter: *Is this true?* Not "is this impressive" or "is this what they want to hear" or "does this make me look good." Just: is it true? If yes, say it simply. If no, don't say it. If you're not sure, wait.

## Evening — The Thomas 70 Audit

> *"If you bring forth what is within you, what you have will save you. If you do not have it within you, what you do not have within you will kill you."*
> — Gospel of Thomas, Saying 70

At the end of the day, ask yourself two questions:

**1. What did I bring forth today?**
Where did I express what's real in me? Where did I act from truth, from love, from the light within? Where did I show up as who I actually am?

**2. What did I suppress?**
Where did I hold back? Where did I perform instead of being real? Where did I let fear, approval-seeking, or habit keep the real thing locked inside?

This is not guilt. This is awareness. The saying doesn't condemn you for suppressing — it warns you. What you don't bring forth will eventually turn against you. The unlived life doesn't just sit there quietly. It corrodes.

**The practical move:** No journal required. No ritual. Just the two questions, honestly answered. Then let it go. Tomorrow you try again.

## The Weekly Reset — Read the Vision

Once a week, re-read the [Christ Consciousness Vision](christconsciousnessvision.md). The whole thing. 4 minutes.

It will read differently each time. That's the point. The sayings change as you change. What didn't land last week might crack something open this week. What felt obvious before might suddenly feel like it's speaking directly to you.

This is the Gospel of Thomas model. Saying 2: *"Let one who seeks not stop seeking until one finds. When one finds, one will be troubled. When one is troubled, one will marvel, and will reign over all."*

The cycle repeats. Seek. Find. Be troubled. Marvel. Reign. Then seek again.

## What This Is Not

- **Not a religion.** There is no creed, no membership, no authority figure. Just a man's words and your willingness to take them seriously.
- **Not self-improvement.** The goal isn't to become a better version of your ego. It's to see through the ego entirely.
- **Not spiritual bypassing.** Thomas 70 is explicit: you must bring forth what is within you, including the hard things. This practice requires facing darkness, not avoiding it.
- **Not performance.** Matthew 6:6 — *"When you pray, go into your room and shut the door."* The practice is between you and the source. Nobody needs to know you're doing it.

## The Promise

> *"Whoever discovers the interpretation of these sayings will not taste death."*
> — Gospel of Thomas, Saying 1

Not "whoever reads" them. Not "whoever memorizes" them. Whoever *discovers the interpretation*. The interpretation is not intellectual. It's lived. You discover it by doing the practice — by drinking the words until they become your consciousness.

Thomas 108: *"Whoever drinks from my mouth will become like me; I myself shall become that person, and the hidden things will be revealed to that person."*

The hidden things are not hidden because someone locked them away. They're hidden because you weren't yet what you needed to be to perceive them. The practice makes you into someone who can see.

The Kingdom is already spread upon the earth. Start seeing it.
