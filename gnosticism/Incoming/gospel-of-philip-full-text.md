# The Gospel of Philip — Full Text (Selected Key Passages)

> A Valentinian Gnostic text from the Nag Hammadi Library (Codex II, Tractate 3). Not a gospel in the narrative sense — a collection of theological reflections, sayings, and meditations on sacraments, knowledge, and the nature of reality.
> Translation: Wesley W. Isenberg in Marvin Meyer, *The Nag Hammadi Scriptures* (HarperOne, 2007). Cross-referenced with Bentley Layton, *The Gnostic Scriptures* (1987).
> Date: Mid-to-late 2nd century CE (Valentinian school).

**Note:** The Gospel of Philip survives in a single damaged manuscript. Gaps exist throughout. The text is not continuous narrative but a loosely organized anthology — closer to a notebook of teachings than a structured gospel.

---

## On Truth and Names

> **Truth did not come into the world naked, but it came in types and images. The world will not receive truth in any other way.** There is a rebirth and an image of rebirth. It is truly necessary to be born again through the image. What is the resurrection? The image must rise again through the image. The bridal chamber and the image must enter through the image into the truth: this is the restoration.

This is one of the most important statements in Gnostic literature. Truth cannot be communicated directly — it must be encoded in symbols, images, types. Every esoteric tradition operates on this principle: the parable, the symbol, the allegory, the ritual are not decorations on truth but the ONLY way truth can enter the material world.

> **Names given to worldly things are very deceptive, for they divert our thoughts from what is correct to what is incorrect.** Thus, one who hears the word "God" does not perceive what is correct but perceives what is incorrect. So also with "the Father" and "the Son" and "the Holy Spirit" and "life" and "light" and "resurrection" and "the church" and all the rest — people do not perceive what is correct but perceive what is incorrect, unless they have come to know what is correct.

Names are veils. The word "God" is not God. The word "resurrection" is not the resurrection. Confusing the name for the reality is the fundamental error. This parallels the Tao Te Ching's opening: "The Tao that can be named is not the eternal Tao."

> The names that are heard in the world are very deceptive. **If they were in the eternal realm, they would at no time be used as names in the world.** Nor were they set among worldly things. They have an end in the eternal realm.

## On Light and Darkness

> **Light and darkness, life and death, right and left, are brothers of one another. They are inseparable.** Because of this, neither are the good good, nor the evil evil, nor is life life, nor death death. For this reason each one will dissolve into its original nature. But those who are exalted above the world are indissoluble, eternal.

Duality is a feature of the material world, not of ultimate reality. Good/evil, light/dark, life/death — these opposites exist only in relation to each other. Above the world of opposites, there is unity. This parallels:
- Hermeticism: The Principle of Polarity — "opposites are identical in nature, but different in degree"
- Kabbalah: The two pillars (Mercy/Severity) resolved in the middle pillar
- Taoism: Yin/yang as interdependent aspects of the Tao
- Plotinus: Evil has no independent existence — it is the privation of good

## On Ignorance and Knowledge

> **Ignorance is the mother of all evil.** Ignorance will result in death, because those who come from ignorance neither were nor are nor shall be. But those who are in the truth will be perfect when all the truth is revealed. For truth is like ignorance: while hidden, it rests in itself; but when it is revealed and recognized, it is praised, inasmuch as it is stronger than ignorance and error. It gives freedom.

> The word says: "If you know the truth, the truth will make you free" (John 8:32). **Ignorance is slavery. Knowledge is freedom.** If we know the truth, we shall find the fruits of the truth within us. If we are joined to it, it will bring our fulfillment.

The diagnosis is identical to the Yoga Sutras: avidya (ignorance) is the root of all affliction. And to Vedanta: avidya (ignorance of one's true nature) is the cause of bondage. Freedom comes through knowledge — not intellectual knowledge, but direct gnosis of reality.

## On the Bridal Chamber

The most distinctive teaching of the Gospel of Philip — the sacrament of the bridal chamber:

> **If anyone becomes a son of the bridal chamber, that person will receive the light.** If anyone does not receive it while in these places, that person will not be able to receive it in the other place. One who receives that light will not be seen, nor can that one be detained. No one will be able to torment such a person, even while living in the world.

> The Lord did everything in a mystery: a baptism and a chrism and a eucharist and a redemption and a bridal chamber.

> **The bridal chamber is in the realm that surpasses it** [...] Those who have been united in the bridal chamber will no longer be separated. Thus Eve separated from Adam because it was not in the bridal chamber that she united with him.

The bridal chamber is not sexual in the literal sense — it is the reunion of the soul with its divine counterpart. The "fall" was the separation of male and female (Adam and Eve). Redemption is their reunion. This parallels:
- Kabbalah: The reunion of Tiferet (the Holy One) and Malkuth (the Shekhinah) — the cosmic marriage that is the goal of tikkun
- Sufism: Fana and baqa — the annihilation of the separate self and its reconstitution in God
- Hinduism: The union of Shiva and Shakti — consciousness and creative power reunited
- Christianity (orthodox): The marriage of Christ and the Church (Ephesians 5:31-32)

## On the Sacraments

> **Those who say they will die first and then rise are in error.** If they do not first receive the resurrection while they live, when they die they will receive nothing.

The resurrection is not a future event after physical death — it is a present reality to be realized NOW, while alive. This is the Gnostic inversion of orthodox eschatology. Parallel to:
- Jesus: "The Kingdom of God is within you" (Luke 17:21) — present, not future
- Vedanta: Liberation (moksha) is realization of what is already true, not attainment of a future state
- Zen: Satori is seeing what has always been the case

> **Baptism is "the holy" building. Redemption is "the holy of the holy." "The holy of the holies" is the bridal chamber.** Baptism includes the resurrection and the redemption, the redemption takes place in the bridal chamber. The bridal chamber is in that which is superior to it and to them. You will find nothing like it.

A three-level sacramental system: baptism (purification) → redemption (liberation) → bridal chamber (union). Maps to initiatory structures across traditions:
- Masonic: Entered Apprentice → Fellow Craft → Master Mason
- Yoga: Yama/Niyama → Dharana/Dhyana → Samadhi
- Kabbalah: Assiah → Yetzirah/Briah → Atziluth

## On Mary Magdalene

> There were three who always walked with the Lord: Mary his mother, her sister, and **Magdalene, the one who was called his companion** (*koinonos*). His sister and his mother and his companion were each a Mary.

> **The companion of the [Savior is] Mary Magdalene. [The Lord loved] her more than [all] the disciples, [and used to] kiss her [often] on her [mouth].** The rest of [the disciples...] They said to him: "Why do you love her more than all of us?" The Savior answered and said to them: "Why do I not love you like her? When a blind man and one who sees are both together in the darkness, they are no different from one another. When the light comes, then the one who sees will see the light, and the one who is blind will remain in darkness."

The word *koinonos* means companion/partner — not necessarily sexual, but indicating a relationship of deep spiritual intimacy and equality. Mary Magdalene is presented as the disciple who sees the light — who has gnosis — while the male disciples remain in the dark.

This passage is significant for the Feminine Divine synthesis: Mary Magdalene as the primary holder of gnosis, the one closest to Christ, the recipient of the "kiss" (spiritual transmission). The jealousy of the male disciples mirrors the institutional suppression of the feminine in later Christianity.

## On the World as Mistake vs. Mystery

> **The world came about through a mistake.** For he who created it wanted to create it imperishable and immortal. He fell short of attaining his desire. For the world never was imperishable, nor, for that matter, was he who made the world.

> For things are not imperishable, but sons and daughters are. Nothing will be able to receive imperishability if it does not first become a son or daughter.

The material world was created by the Demiurge who wanted it to be eternal but failed. The world is therefore impermanent by nature. But within it, the divine spark (the "son" or "daughter") can attain what the world itself cannot — imperishability through gnosis.

## On the Holy Spirit and the Feminine

> **Some said Mary conceived by the holy Spirit. They are in error. They do not know what they are saying. When did a woman ever conceive by a woman?** The holy Spirit is female. Mary is the virgin whom no power defiled.

In the Gospel of Philip, the Holy Spirit is explicitly feminine (*Ruach* is feminine in Hebrew). Mary conceived not by a female Spirit literally, but through the divine creative power that transcends gender. The text is correcting a misunderstanding: the virgin birth is a spiritual reality, not a biological one.

This is significant: the Holy Spirit as feminine aligns with:
- Kabbalah: The Shekhinah as the feminine presence of God
- Gnosticism: Barbelo / Sophia as the feminine divine
- Hinduism: Shakti as the creative power

## On Knowledge vs. Love

> **Love builds up** (1 Corinthians 8:1). In fact, whoever is truly free through knowledge is a slave because of love for those who have not yet been able to attain the freedom of knowledge. Knowledge makes them capable of becoming free.

> **Love never calls anything its own** [...] Love does not say "this is yours" or "this is mine" [...] It says, "all these are yours."

Knowledge (gnosis) liberates the individual. But love then compels the liberated person to serve those still bound. Knowledge without love is incomplete. This resolves the tension between the Gnostic emphasis on knowledge and the Christian emphasis on love — they are not opposites but a sequence: knowledge frees, love serves.

## On the Pearl in the Mud

> **If a pearl is cast down into mud, it is not thereby held in less esteem.** Nor if it is anointed with balsam oil will it be more valued. But it always has value in the eyes of its owner. Compare the sons and daughters of God wherever they may be. They still have value in the eyes of their Father.

The divine spark within cannot be diminished by its circumstances. Whether in the mud of the material world or anointed in a temple — the pearl is the pearl. Your true nature is unchanged by where you are. Parallel to:
- Vedanta: Atman is untouched by the body and its conditions
- Zen: Buddha-nature is present in every being without exception
- Law of One: Each entity contains the Creator in full, regardless of density or circumstance

## On the Purpose of the World

> **Glass decanters and earthenware jugs are both made by means of fire.** But if glass decanters break, they are done over, for they came into being through a breath. If earthenware jugs break, they are destroyed, for they came into being without breath.

> A donkey going round and round a millstone walked a hundred miles. When it was loosed, it found that it was still in the same place.

The world is the millstone. Without gnosis, you walk endlessly in circles — a hundred miles, still in the same place. Reincarnation without awakening is the donkey at the mill. Gnosis is the loosening that lets you see where you actually are.

## On the End

> **In this world, those who put on garments are better than the garments. In the kingdom of heaven, the garments are better than those who have put them on.**

> By water and fire the whole place is purified — the visible by the visible, the hidden by the hidden. There are some things hidden through those visible. There is water in water, there is fire in chrism.

> **Blessed is the one who is before coming into being.** For the one who is, was and shall be.
