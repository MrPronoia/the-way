## The Apocryphon of John (Secret Book of John)

**What it is:** The foundational Gnostic cosmology. Four copies found at Nag Hammadi — the most-copied text, indicating central importance. A revelation dialogue: the risen Christ appears to John on a mountain and reveals the true structure of reality.

**School:** Sethian Gnosticism
**Date:** Mid-2nd century CE (possibly earlier core material)
**Length:** ~15 pages (longer version, Codex II)

### The Story in One Paragraph

The true God (the Monad / Invisible Spirit) is utterly beyond all names, attributes, and comprehension. From this God, the first thought — Barbelo, the divine Mother-Father — emanates. From Barbelo comes Autogenes (the Christ / divine Mind). Together they generate the Pleroma (divine fullness) — a realm of light populated by aeons. One of these aeons, **Sophia** (Wisdom), acts without her consort's consent and produces a deformed offspring: **Yaldabaoth**, the Demiurge. Ignorant of the realms above, Yaldabaoth declares "I am God and there is no other God beside me" and creates the material world and its archon rulers. But when his archons create a human body (Adam), they cannot give it life. The divine realm tricks Yaldabaoth into breathing his stolen light into Adam — planting a divine spark in every human. The archons then trap humans in matter and forgetfulness. Christ descends to awaken the spark: "Rise up, remember that you have heard. Trace back your root to me."

### Key Teachings (6)

| # | Teaching | Quote / Summary |
|---|---------|----------------|
| 1 | **God is beyond all description** | "Not corporeal. Not incorporeal. Not large. Not small. Not a quantity. Not a creature. No one can think of it." Negative theology — God defined by what God is NOT. |
| 2 | **The first emanation is feminine** | Barbelo — "the Mother-Father, the first Human, the holy Spirit, the triple-named androgynous one." The first thought of God is both male and female. |
| 3 | **The material world results from error** | Sophia acts without consent → produces the Demiurge → who creates a flawed world believing he is the highest God. Creation is a mistake, not a plan. |
| 4 | **The biblical God is the Demiurge** | "I am God and there is no other God beside me" (Isaiah 45:5) — the Gnostics invert this. The God who says this is the *ignorant* creator, not the true God above. |
| 5 | **A divine spark is hidden in every human** | "The luminous Epinoia was hidden in Adam, so that the archons might not know about it." Your real identity is this spark — not the body the archons made. |
| 6 | **Salvation = remembering your origin** | "I am the remembrance of the Pleroma." Christ doesn't save through sacrifice — he saves through *reminding*. Gnosis is anamnesis (un-forgetting). |

## The Gospel of Philip

**What it is:** A Valentinian anthology of theological reflections, sayings, and meditations on sacraments, knowledge, and the nature of reality. Not a narrative gospel — more like a notebook of teachings. Survives in a single damaged manuscript with gaps throughout.

**School:** Valentinian Gnosticism (the most philosophically sophisticated Gnostic school)
**Date:** Mid-to-late 2nd century CE
**Length:** ~12 pages

### The Big Ideas (10)

| # | Teaching | Key Quote |
|---|---------|-----------|
| 1 | **Truth can only be communicated through symbols** | "Truth did not come into the world naked, but it came in types and images. The world will not receive truth in any other way." Every parable, ritual, symbol — not decoration on truth but the ONLY vehicle for truth in the material world. |
| 2 | **Names are veils, not reality** | "Names given to worldly things are very deceptive... one who hears the word 'God' does not perceive what is correct." Confusing the name for the reality is the fundamental error. Cf. "The Tao that can be named is not the eternal Tao." |
| 3 | **Duality is a feature of the material world only** | "Light and darkness, life and death, right and left, are brothers of one another. They are inseparable... those who are exalted above the world are indissoluble, eternal." Above the world of opposites: unity. |
| 4 | **Ignorance is the root of all evil** | "Ignorance is the mother of all evil... Ignorance is slavery. Knowledge is freedom." Identical to Vedanta's *avidya* (ignorance) as the root of bondage, and Buddhism's *avijja* as the first link of dependent origination. |
| 5 | **The resurrection is NOW, not after death** | "Those who say they will die first and then rise are in error. If they do not first receive the resurrection while they live, when they die they will receive nothing." The Gnostic inversion of orthodox eschatology. |
| 6 | **The bridal chamber is the highest sacrament** | Three levels: Baptism (purification) → Redemption (liberation) → Bridal Chamber (union). The bridal chamber = reunion of the soul with its divine counterpart. The "fall" was separation; redemption is reunion. |
| 7 | **Mary Magdalene as primary disciple** | "The companion of the Savior is Mary Magdalene. The Lord loved her more than all the disciples." *Koinonos* = companion/partner. She has gnosis; the male disciples remain in the dark. |
| 8 | **The Holy Spirit is feminine** | "When did a woman ever conceive by a woman? The holy Spirit is female." *Ruach* is feminine in Hebrew. Aligns with Shekinah (Kabbalah), Sophia/Barbelo (Gnosticism), Shakti (Hinduism). |
| 9 | **Knowledge frees; love serves** | "Whoever is truly free through knowledge is a slave because of love for those who have not yet been able to attain the freedom of knowledge." Knowledge and love are not opposites but a sequence. |
| 10 | **The divine spark is untouched by circumstances** | "If a pearl is cast down into mud, it is not thereby held in less esteem." Your true nature is unchanged by where you are. = Atman untouched by the body (Vedanta), Buddha-nature in every being (Zen). |

### The Donkey at the Millstone

> "A donkey going round and round a millstone walked a hundred miles. When it was loosed, it found that it was still in the same place."

The world is the millstone. Without gnosis, you walk endlessly in circles — reincarnation without awakening. Gnosis is the loosening.

## Both Texts Together — The Gnostic Core

What these two texts establish when read together:

1. **The true God is beyond all names and concepts** — not the creator of this world
2. **The material world results from a cosmic error** — Sophia's unauthorized act
3. **The biblical creator (Yaldabaoth) is ignorant** — not evil, but blind
4. **A divine spark is hidden in every human** — placed there through cosmic trickery
5. **Archons maintain ignorance** — the system keeps you asleep
6. **Truth can only be transmitted through symbols** — direct statement is impossible
7. **Gnosis (direct knowing) is the only salvation** — not faith, not works, not ritual
8. **The resurrection is a present reality** — not a future event after death
9. **The feminine divine is central** — Barbelo, Sophia, the Holy Spirit, Mary Magdalene
10. **The path is remembering** — you don't become something new; you remember what you are

### Where Gnosticism Agrees with the Perennial Philosophy

- Divine spark within (Tier 1 — unanimous)
- Inner transformation over external ritual (Tier 2 — strong)
- The material world is not ultimate reality (Tier 2 — strong)
- Direct knowing over belief (across all mystical traditions)
- Emanation structure (Tier 3 — the Pleroma maps structurally)
- Three-stage path (baptism → redemption → bridal chamber)
- The feminine divine as essential (feeds the synthesis in Move 7)

### Where Gnosticism Diverges

- **The world is a mistake** — most traditions see creation as purposeful (lila, tzimtzum, the Creator knowing itself). Gnosticism alone says the world was an accident by an ignorant deity.
- **The creator is the enemy** — Plotinus explicitly rejected this (Enneads II.9). Kabbalah says repair the world, not escape it. Law of One says physical experience is a valuable gift.
- **Escape, not repair** — Gnostics want OUT. Kabbalists want TIKKUN (repair). Hindus see LILA (play). The Gnostic response to matter is uniquely negative among the traditions in this repo.
