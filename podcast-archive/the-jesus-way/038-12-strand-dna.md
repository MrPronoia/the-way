## Summary

This episode explores the intersection of spiritual science and biological evolution through the lens of Dr. Samuel Lee's teachings on 12-strand DNA activation. Dr. Lee, an integrative psychiatrist who left a partnership at one of LA's biggest psychiatric hospital firms to pursue spiritual healing, shares his journey from being a Seventh-day Adventist pastor's kid to discovering what he calls "the freedom teachings" - a framework suggesting that humans have dormant DNA strands (beyond the two physical strands) that correspond to soul, oversoul, and avatar/Christos consciousness levels.

He presents a base-12 mathematical system as the "eternal life code" (contrasting with base-10 systems like Kabbalah), arguing that 12 represents continuous regenerative energy while 10 represents finite/consumptive energy patterns.

The hosts and guest discuss how Jesus likely gained his abilities during his "lost years" (age 12-30) through training in India and Tibet, learning to merge with the elements and activate higher DNA strands. They emphasize that the purpose of these teachings is ultimately simple: authentic self-expression, presence, love, and trust in God - which Dr. Lee calls "divinity is simplicity."
