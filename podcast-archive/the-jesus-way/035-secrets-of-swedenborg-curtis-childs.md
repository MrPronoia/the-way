## Summary

This episode features Curtis Childs, host of the "Off The Left Eye" YouTube channel, discussing the profound teachings of 18th-century mystic Emanuel Swedenborg. The conversation explores Swedenborg's revolutionary ideas about the afterlife, heaven and hell, the nature of God, and true Christology. Key themes include: heaven and hell as states of mind that manifest externally; the universal nature of salvation based on how one lives rather than doctrinal beliefs; Swedenborg's unique understanding of the Trinity as aspects of one God (soul, body, actions); and the purpose of Jesus's incarnation as God walking the human path to show us the way. The episode challenges traditional Christian doctrines about eternal conscious torment, blood atonement, and faith-alone salvation while affirming the centrality of Jesus's teachings about loving God and neighbor.

## Key Teachings

### Who Was Emanuel Swedenborg
- 18th-century Swedish scientist, inventor, and mystic who published across multiple fields (anatomy, botany, chemistry)
- Founded crystallography as a scientific branch
- Designed early prototypes of submarines and flying machines
- Had a foundational encounter with Jesus Christ in 1745 in the back room of a London pub
- Claimed to have access to the spiritual world daily for the rest of his life
- Wrote 30 volumes over three decades documenting his spiritual experiences
- Influenced William Blake, Rudolf Steiner, Helen Keller, Carl Jung, and Bill Wilson (12 Steps)

### Heaven and Hell as States of Mind
- **Heaven** = The pleasures of wisdom; love of doing good to others
- **Hell** = The pleasures of insanity; love of harming/exploiting others for personal gain
- Both are fundamentally about what you LOVE at your core
- "What you love deep down... makes you who you are"
- The afterlife is governed by spiritual laws just as the physical world is governed by physical laws
- In the spiritual world, love functions like gravity - you gravitate to what you love

### The Two Polarities
- **Service to Others (Heaven)**: Joy in benefiting others, serving humanity, creating win-win situations
- **Service to Self (Hell)**: Joy in dominating, crushing, revenge, exploitation
- Evil is seductive and feels good temporarily (pleasures of insanity)
- Good requires discipline but leads to lasting joy (pleasures of wisdom)
- "Temporary pleasure leads to long-term pain; temporary pain leads to long-term pleasure"

### Salvation and the Afterlife
- Heaven is NOT exclusive to Christians
- "In the other world... nobody asks what your religion was like. They ask what your life was like"
- Swedenborg saw Muslims, Jews, and people of all faiths in heaven
- The essentials: Don't do evil, believe in God as the source of good, try to live accordingly
- No one is sent to hell by God - people gravitate there based on what they love
- Hell exists as God's accommodation for those who choose selfishness - giving them the best life possible given their choices

### The "World of Spirits" (Initial Afterlife)
- Upon death, everyone goes through a sorting process
- Like a centrifuge separating what's truly inside you
- You get to discover and decide: Will you cling to good or evil?
- Your "ruling love" (what you love above all) determines your ultimate destination
- Those in hell aren't tortured by God - they're there because they WANT to be there pursuing their dark pleasures

### The Trinity Reimagined
- NOT three separate persons with separate wills
- One God with three aspects (like human mind, body, and actions):
  - **Father** = The soul/divine essence (infinite, unknowable)
  - **Son** = The manifestation we can understand (God made relatable)
  - **Holy Spirit** = Divine providence/action proceeding into the world
- Jesus saying "Father is greater than I" was during his periods of "exinanition" (emptying) when he experienced human limitations
- "Glorification" = When Jesus manifested full divine power ("I and the Father are one")

### Why Jesus Incarnated
- Humanity was choosing evil so extensively that hell was overpowering heaven's influence
- "Total destruction stood at the gates"
- God needed to become "attackable" - take on human vulnerability to conquer evil from within
- Jesus walked a "mega version" of the human path, facing all temptations we face
- Through this, he "subdued hell" and created a new way for humans to connect to God
- The crucifixion was Jesus as the "ultimate prophetic demonstration" - showing what humanity does to God's word

### On Paul
- Paul had ego issues both before and after conversion
- Swedenborg encountered Paul in the afterlife still dealing with desire for power/prestige
- Paul's writings were providentially kept separate from Jesus's teachings so corruptions wouldn't "pollute" Christ's words
- Paul was useful for kickstarting Christianity but had significant limitations
- The other apostles rejected Paul according to Swedenborg

## Misconceptions Addressed

### 1. "You must be Christian to go to heaven"
**Correction:** Heaven is for anyone who loves what God loves - doing good to others. Swedenborg saw Muslims, Jews, and people of all faiths in heaven. "Nobody asks what your religion was like. They ask what your life was like."

### 2. "Hell is eternal conscious torment as punishment"
**Correction:** Hell is people choosing to pursue selfish pleasures; God tries to give them the best life possible given their choices. There's no revenge from God. Angels work in hell trying to help people improve. Anyone could leave if they changed what they love.

### 3. "Salvation comes through belief alone"
**Correction:** "Good works" matter because they reflect what you truly love. Believing correct doctrines without living them is worthless. Jesus constantly urged people to DO things - repent, forgive, love - not just believe facts.

### 4. "The Trinity is three separate persons"
**Correction:** One God with three aspects - like soul, body, and actions in a human. Father/Son conversations in Gospels reflect Jesus's process of "exinanition" (human vulnerability) and "glorification" (divine breakthrough).

### 5. "Heaven and hell are external places you get sent to"
**Correction:** They are primarily states of mind/heart that then manifest as experiences. "The heaven someone experiences outwardly is in keeping with the heaven within them."

### 6. "God's love is conditional/excludes sinners"
**Correction:** "Any angel would gladly trade places with anyone in hell if they wanted to." God loves everyone in hell too. The separation comes from the person, not God.

### 7. "Paul's writings are equal to Jesus's teachings"
**Correction:** Swedenborg distinguishes between the Gospels (which have infinite spiritual depth) and epistles (good books but not "the Word" with inner meaning). Paul's ego issues affected his writings.
