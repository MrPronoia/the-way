## Summary

**One-sentence summary:** Christian mystic John St. Julien recounts building special-needs orphanages in Tanzania, the witch-doctor child sacrifice he confronts, and how surrendered innocence is the heart of Jesus's teaching.

### Key Arguments
1. **Surrender over self-doership is the core of walking with Christ** — letting "divine intelligence animate the body without an inner narrative," citing Jesus ("I can only do what I see the Father doing") and a decades-long "reading ban" of sitting in God's presence rather than studying theology.
2. **Faith grows incrementally through assumed responsibility** — each child made the next feel manageable ("two felt like one, four like two"), building faith exponentially.
3. **Special-needs child abuse in Tanzania is driven by poverty, superstition, and survival-over-empathy** — children seen as cursed/burdens, hidden, chained, or starved.
4. **A real "destruction of innocence" spirit operates identically across cultures** — links Tanzanian witch-doctor/albino sacrifice to the Epstein/"Cabal" phenomenon; the *friction* of destroyed innocence is the sought-after fuel.
5. **The blood-atonement doctrine makes God want what Satan wants** — Abke argues that claiming God required an innocent man's violent death puts God in the same negative polarity as every dark tradition; "Jesus died because of love."
6. **Spiritual warfare manifests physically** — a 2024 infiltration plot, a hitman tip-off, a staged break-in, an exploding transformer during a "density" he attributes to spell-casting.
7. **Deliverance works by drawing distorted energies into one's own purified field** — praying in tongues, he and his wife Fritzie pull attachments off abused children; reframes the pigs of Gadara as a *mercy*.
8. **Sin loosens your "frequency band" and grants entities access** — sin (broadly "missing the mark") lengthens the wavelength and creates attachment points.
9. **Innocence + truth are non-negotiable and the heart of mysticism** — "unless you become as children"; radical financial transparency (per-dollar traceable donations) as truth-anchoring.
