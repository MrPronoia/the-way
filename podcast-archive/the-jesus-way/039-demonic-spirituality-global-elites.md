2. **"Animal sacrifice was okay because God commanded it"** - Blood sacrifice predates Judaism by thousands of years as a pagan practice. The prophets consistently condemned it (Hosea, Isaiah, Jeremiah, Amos, Micah).

3. **"God's ways are higher than our ways" (to justify divine violence)** - This inverts the truth. Humans are violent; God is love. If our ways are mercy and forgiveness, then God's "higher ways" would be MORE merciful, not more violent.

4. **"The disciples and Paul were unified"** - Paul admits he didn't receive his gospel from them, mocks them as "so-called pillars," and there's evidence of significant conflict. The Nazarenes/Ebionites rejected Paul entirely.

5. **"Animals don't matter to God"** - If animals don't matter, how can their blood "pay" for human sin? Jesus said God cares even when a sparrow falls.

6. **"The wages of sin is death, so God needed a substitute"** - This creates a logical absurdity: God should have to torture Jesus eternally (not just a weekend) to pay for eternal punishment. The entire transaction metaphor is incoherent.

7. **"Matthew/Mark/Luke/John were written by eyewitnesses contemporaneously"** - These were written decades later; Mark is based on Paul's theology; Matthew's blood atonement Eucharist language was a later addition not found in Paul or Mark.

8. **"Christianity was unified until heretics came along"** - There was massive diversity from the beginning; what became "orthodox" was actually Paul's version, canonized by the Roman state centuries later.
