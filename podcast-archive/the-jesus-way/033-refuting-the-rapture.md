## Summary

In this episode, Aaron sits down with James, Kam, and returning guest Chris Sartain to discuss the teachings of Jesus on the "end times" and whether Jesus really had "failed prophecies" about the apocalypse. The episode introduces **participatory eschatology** - the concept that Jesus taught humans are co-creators of God's kingdom on earth rather than passive observers waiting for divine intervention. This directly contrasts with modern Christianity's escapist, fear-based eschatology that pictures Jesus returning on a war horse to slay enemies. The panel argues that Jesus's kingdom teachings emphasize present-moment participation through works, love, and service - not future rapture or apocalyptic warfare. The episode draws connections between ancient teachings (Didache, Isaiah 11, Gospel of Thomas) and modern concepts like the Maharishi Effect and morphic resonance, suggesting humanity can collectively manifest the "Edenic ideal" through righteous living.

## Key Teachings

### Participatory Eschatology vs. Apocalyptic Eschatology
- **Participatory eschatology**: Active involvement in shaping the future rather than passive waiting for divine intervention
- Jesus taught the kingdom was "already present, already now, at hand, and within us"
- Modern Christian eschatology is "entirely escapist, built on fear and doom"
- The depiction of "Rambo Jesus" returning with a sword sounds more like a Roman emperor than a humble Hebrew rabbi
- Christianity's pro-war tendencies stem from expecting a final battle to end the world

### The Kingdom is NOW
- "Repent for the kingdom of heaven is at hand" - an eschatological claim about present reality
- Gospel of Thomas: "The kingdom will not come by waiting for it"
- Kingdom is spread across the earth but "men are unaware of it"
- Luke 17:21: "The kingdom of heaven is within you"
- Luke 4: Jesus reads Isaiah scroll and says "Today this scripture is fulfilled in your hearing"

### Three Interpretations of the Kingdom
1. **Heavenly afterlife realm** - Not Jesus's primary emphasis
2. **State of consciousness/enlightenment** - Found in Gospel of Thomas
3. **Participatory co-creation** - The primary teaching - we manifest the kingdom through works

### Jesus as Catalyst, We as Co-Creators
- Jesus was the "impetus" or "catalyst" for the kingdom's manifestation
- "I have set you an example that you should also do as I have done to you" (John 13:15)
- "A servant is not greater than his master" - if Jesus followed the law, so should we
- The mustard seed parables show the kingdom starts small but grows through participation

### The Second Coming as Collective Awakening
- Chris Sartain proposes the "second coming" is happening NOW through:
  - Rediscovery of Gospel of Thomas (1940s)
  - Piecing together of Hebrew Gospel
  - Popularization of Clementine literature
  - The neo-Essene, neo-Nazarene, neo-Ebionite movement
- When the son of man comes, "it will be like lightning flashing from east to west" - a metaphor for collective planetary awakening
- Mass consciousness shift, not individual bodily return

### The Maharishi Effect and Morphic Resonance
- **Maharishi Effect**: Reaching critical mass of meditating individuals shifts collective planetary consciousness
- **Hundredth Monkey Effect**: When enough members of a species learn a skill, it spreads to entire population regardless of distance
- **Rupert Sheldrake's morphic resonance**: Information exchange through collective fields
- **David Hawkins scale**: One person at level 500 (love) offsets 750,000 people below 200; one at 600+ offsets 10 million
- Ancient lamad vav tzadikim tradition: At least 36 extremely righteous people on earth at all times, offsetting evil

### The Lord's Prayer as Kingdom Blueprint
- From the Didache (earliest version, ~70 AD)
- "Thy kingdom come, thy will be done on earth as in heaven" = participatory eschatology embedded in prayer
- Kingdom manifests when God's will is done on earth - through us
- Didache instructs: pray this three times daily
- Each line serves a purpose:
  - Acknowledging God's nature
  - Participatory kingdom building
  - Gratitude for provision
  - Forgiveness practice
  - Protection from temptation

### Works-Based Soteriology
- James: "Be doers of the word, not merely hearers"
- "Faith apart from works is worthless"
- Paul's "faith-based soteriology" doesn't advance kingdom manifestation
- "Even the demons believe" - belief alone accomplishes nothing
- Jesus in Matthew 7: "Not everyone who says 'Lord, Lord' will enter the kingdom, but only the one who does the will of my Father"

### Isaiah 11 and Return to Edenic Ideal
- Prophecy of the branch (netzer = root of "Nazarene") from Jesse's stump
- "The wolf shall live with the lamb"
- "They will not hurt or destroy on all my holy mountain"
- Lions eating straw like oxen = carnivores becoming herbivores
- This is achievable - dogs (wolves) now thrive on plant-based diets
- Polar bears adapting to more plant foods as environment changes

### Origins of Flesh Eating
- Clementine Homilies: Flesh eating taught by Nephilim (hybrid demon/human race)
- Eating animals is "contrary to nature"
- Original Edenic diet was fully plant-based (Genesis 1:29)
- Jerome (church father): Eating flesh "was unknown until the deluge"
- Flesh eating was a "concession to human hardness of heart" like divorce

### Mark 13 and "Failed Prophecies"
- Jesus likely did prophesy temple destruction (historically accurate - 70 AD)
- Apocalyptic additions (Daniel 7, son of man on clouds) are later interpolations
- Mark 13 closely mirrors post-70 AD events = written after the fact
- Editorial shaping by later writers mixing Paul's eschatology with Jesus's sayings
- "This generation will not pass" likely referred to temple destruction, not world's end
