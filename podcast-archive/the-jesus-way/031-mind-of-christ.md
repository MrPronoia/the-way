# Episode 31: The Mind of Christ: Unlocking "Christ Vision" and "Kingdom Consciousness"

## Summary

This episode explores two core concepts for living the Jesus Way: Christ Vision and Kingdom Consciousness. Aaron and James argue that all evils in the world stem from distorted perception, and that learning to see as Christ sees is fundamental to Yeshua's gospel. They define Christ Vision as the ability to perceive every person as a divine being worthy of unconditional love, seeing beyond the physical body to the eternal spiritual reality within. Kingdom Consciousness refers to awareness of God's eternal laws functioning in reality and the understanding that we already exist in a perfect universe that God created.

The hosts ground their teaching in A Course in Miracles (ACIM), which defines a "miracle" as a shift in perception from fear to love. They emphasize that Jesus's true gospel centered on forgiveness, not blood atonement, with unforgiveness being the "true unpardonable sin." The episode revisits their foundational teaching of the "Five O's" - the five ontological qualities of God: Oneness, Omnipotence, Omniscience, Omnipresence, and Omnibenevolence. These serve as a hermeneutical standard for interpreting scripture and shields against the ego's distorted perceptions.

Throughout the episode, James plays "devil's advocate," raising challenging questions about suffering, evil, and how to maintain Christ Vision in hostile situations. Aaron responds with nuanced explanations about the difference between metaphysical truth and practical compassion, the privation theory of evil (evil as absence rather than presence), and the importance of humility in the spiritual path. The teaching emphasizes that perception is creation - if humanity continues to perceive evil and brokenness, we will keep creating it, but through forgiveness and Christ Vision, we can become "saviors in our world."

## Key Teachings

- **Christ Vision** is how we see others - perceiving every person as a divine being, a child of God, worthy of unconditional love, regardless of their actions
- **Kingdom Consciousness** is awareness that we already exist in a perfect universe operating under God's eternal laws
- **A miracle (per ACIM)** is a shift in perception from fear to love, not an outward demonstration
- **Perception is creation** - how we perceive reality directly shapes what we create and experience
- **Heaven is entered two by two** - the only way to access the kingdom is through the person right in front of you; if you see them as a sinner, you shut the door in your own face
- **The "single eye"** that Jesus taught refers to spiritual perception, not physical vision
- **Evil is privation** - evil is the absence of reality (like cold is absence of heat), not a competing power against God
- **Forgiveness is the true atonement** - Jesus taught that if you do not forgive others, you cannot be forgiven
- **Two powers do not exist** - there is no good versus evil dualism in God's universe; what we call evil is the ignorant creation of man
- **The Five O's** (ontological qualities of God):
  1. **Oneness** - God is changeless and indivisible, cannot be separated into parts
  2. **Omnipotence** - God possesses all power; you cannot give God anything or change God's state
  3. **Omniscience** - God possesses all knowledge; if there's a problem, God has the answer
  4. **Omnipresence** - God is never absent from your experience; you've never had an experience apart from God
  5. **Omnibenevolence** - Everything God does is for your highest good; God cannot do harm
- **Guilt, shame, and condemnation are not of God** - they are tools of the negative polarity, never the positive
- **Catching yourself judging is cause for celebration** - awareness of ego activity means you're standing in your Christ nature
- **Practical application remains important** - metaphysical truths should never be used to dismiss real human suffering or act impractically
- **Greek word "pisteo" (believe)** means to trust, commit oneself to, or obey - not mere mental assent to a concept
- **The ego serves as an anchor point for negative entities** - humility and egolessness provide no attachment points for demonic influence
