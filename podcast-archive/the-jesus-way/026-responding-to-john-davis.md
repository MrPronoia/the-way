## Summary

In this episode, the Jesus Way trio (Aaron, James, and Kameron) responds to criticism from John Davis, a new age teacher who claims to be the reincarnation of the Apostle John. Davis had made multiple videos attacking the Jesus Way message after being uninvited from their upcoming summit due to philosophical differences on Jesus's Essene connections and vegetarianism.

The episode systematically addresses Davis's claims that:
- There is "no historical evidence" for Jesus's Essene connections
- Jesus was not a vegetarian
- The Theosophical Society invented these ideas in the early 1900s
- Jesus never claimed to be the Messiah
- The Nazarenes simply means "followers of Jesus from Nazareth"

The hosts present extensive historical attestations from church fathers, first-century historians, Dead Sea Scrolls, and New Testament texts to demonstrate the overwhelming scholarly evidence for Jesus emerging from "Essene-type Judaism" and the early Christian commitment to non-violence toward animals.

## Key Teachings

### The "Jesua" Problem
- John Davis claims Jesus was called "Jesua" with a J sound
- The letter J did not exist in Hebrew, Aramaic, Greek, or Latin in Jesus's time
- J is a medieval English invention from around the 14th century
- In Hebrew: Yeshua or Yahashua; in Aramaic: Issa
- This anachronistic claim undermines Davis's credibility as someone with "past life memories"

### Essene-Type Judaism vs. Card-Carrying Essenism
- Not claiming Jesus was a "card-carrying Essene" living in Qumran caves
- "Essene-type Judaism" was a widespread movement with multiple sects
- Two main branches by Jesus's day: Nazarenes (north) and Ossenes (south)
- Jesus emerged from the northern Nazarene branch

### 11 Unique Parallels Between Essenes and Jesus Movement
1. **Atonement baptism** - forgiveness of sins through repentance and water (not practiced by Pharisees/Sadducees)
2. **Imminent apocalypticism** - belief in imminent end times
3. **Led by Teacher of Righteousness** - Jesus called "teacher from God" and "the righteous one"
4. **12 council members** - 12 disciples
5. **3 appointed priests** - James, Peter, and John (mentioned 8 times together)
6. **People of New Covenant** - anti-sacrifice covenant
7. **Forbidding divorce** - Jesus forbids divorce in Luke 16
8. **Voluntary poverty** - "sell all you possess to the poor"
9. **Opposing animal sacrifices** - "I desire mercy, not sacrifice"
10. **Rejecting orthodox liturgical laws** - "You abandon God's commandments for human tradition"
11. **Favoring Isaiah and Jeremiah** - Jesus references Isaiah dozens of times, Jeremiah six times

### The Ebionite/Ebonyim Connection
- Dead Sea Scroll self-designation: "congregation of the Ebionim" (the poor ones)
- Early church fathers confirm: "All Christians were called Nazarenes once"
- Jesus's invitation: "Sell all you possess to the Ebion (the poor), then follow me"
- Jerome interprets Paul's "remember the poor" as referring to the Ebion community

### Vegetarianism Evidence
From first-century historians:
- **Josephus** on Essenes: "Their food consists of loaves of bread, a dish of vegetables with salt, and water"
- **Philo**: "They do not bring sacrifices of animals"
- **Porphyry**: "The Essenes abstain from all animal food"

From church fathers:
- **Jerome**: "Jesus joined the end to the beginning so we are no longer allowed to eat flesh"
- **Hegesippus** on James: "Holy from his mother's womb. He drank no wine... nor did he eat meat"
- **Clement of Alexandria** on Matthew: "Partook of seeds and nuts, hard-shelled fruits and vegetables without flesh"
- **Eusebius**: The 12 disciples "embraced a laborious life with fasting and abstinence from wine and meat"

### Hebrew Gospel of Matthew Quotes
- "I have come to abolish the sacrifices. And if you cease not from sacrificing, the wrath of God will not cease from you"
- "I have not desired to eat flesh in this Passover with you"
- This gospel is attested by over a dozen church fathers as written by Matthew himself (dated 33-50 AD)

### Jesus's Messianic Claims (Refuting Davis)
- Mark 14: "Are you the Messiah?" Jesus: "I am"
- John 4:25-26: "I am he, the one speaking to you"
- John 10: "I have told you and you do not believe"
- Matthew 16: Peter's confession - "Blessed are you, for my Father revealed this"
- Jesus seminar scholars agree: Messianic claims are among the most historically verified facts about Jesus

### The Beloved Disciple Question
- Most scholars do NOT believe John son of Zebedee was the beloved disciple
- Dr. James Tabor argues it was James the brother of Jesus:
  - "Mother, behold your son" - Jewish custom of eldest brother assuming family headship
  - The beloved disciple enters the high priest's house (James had temple access)
  - John 21 distinguishes "sons of Zebedee" from "the beloved disciple"
  - The Zebedee sons' two recorded actions: wanting to burn a village, asking for highest positions

### Nazarene Etymology
- Does NOT mean "follower of Jesus from Nazareth"
- Hebrew root: Notorim = "keepers of the covenant"
- Pliny the Elder (50 AD using records from 30 BC): "Tetrarchy of the Nazarini" in southern Syria/Trans-Jordan
- Epiphanius: "The Nazarene sect was before Christ"
- Gospel of Philip: Nazarene as "keeper/revealer of truth" (not geographical)

## Misconceptions Addressed

### "No Historical Evidence for Essene-Jesus Connection"
Refuted with 11 unique parallels found only in Essene-type Judaism and the Jesus movement, plus extensive attestations from Josephus, Philo, Pliny, Dead Sea Scrolls, and church fathers.

### "Theosophical Society Invented Vegetarian Jesus"
The hosts have never studied or cited the Theosophical Society. Vegetarianism traces back thousands of years to Hinduism, Pythagoreans (500 years before Jesus), and is attested for Essenes by first-century historians.

### "Dead Sea Scrolls Are Only Torah Copies"
The scrolls contain dozens of sectarian documents: commentaries on prophets, liturgical documents, thanksgiving hymns, and community rules that scholars use to connect the Ebionim to the Jesus movement.

### "Essenes Were Extreme Torah Followers"
The Essenes specifically rejected temple sacrifice and believed the Torah had been corrupted by "the lying pen of the scribes" (Jeremiah 8:8). The Damascus Document accuses temple priests of defiling the temple.

### "Jesus Never Claimed to Be the Messiah"
Seven direct messianic claims exist in the Gospels. This is one of the most historically verified facts according to the Jesus Seminar.

### "Nazarene = From Nazareth"
The Nazarene sect predated Jesus (Epiphanius). Pliny documents the "Nazarini" in 30 BC. The Hebrew root "notorim" means "keepers of the covenant."

### "The Essenes Wanted a Military King"
The Essenes were the most pacifistic, anti-militaristic group of their day. They refused to bear arms, had no weapons in their encampments, and believed angels would fight for them.
