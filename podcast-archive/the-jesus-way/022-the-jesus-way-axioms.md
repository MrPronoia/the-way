## Summary

In this foundational episode, Aaron and James present "The 10 Jesus Way Axioms" - a comprehensive distillation of Yeshua's core teachings in axiomatic form. These 10 principles serve as both a personal guide for living the Jesus Way and a resource for explaining the philosophy to others. ## Key Teachings: The 10 Jesus Way Axioms

### Axiom 1: The Golden Rule (Greatest Commandment)
**Statement:** Love God with all your heart, soul, mind, and strength. And the second is like it: Love your neighbor as yourself. No commandment is greater than this.

**Sources:** Matthew 22:37-40, Mark 12:30-31, Gospel of Thomas

**Key Points:**
- Foundation of everything taught on the Jesus Way
- Epistemic grounding for all beliefs and practices
- If something violates the golden rule, it is rejected - whether in scripture, theology, or tradition
- "Love your neighbor as yourself" fulfills the entire law and the prophets
- Includes healthy love of self as integral to loving others
- Love as a trinity: Love God, love others, love yourself - all three feed each other

### Axiom 2: Sin is That Which Causes Harm
**Statement:** Sin is the violation of love. To love is to do no harm.

**Sources:** Matthew 5:21-22, Matthew 7:12, James 4:17

**Key Points:**
- Sin is not an ambiguous idea or a wrong belief - sin is a harmful action
- Harm = violating the free will or wellbeing of another
- Philosophically: Free will requires allowing all beings to do whatever they want unless they infringe on others
- Karma as consequence: You must experience the harm you cause
- The universe exists in perfect balance; attempting to "harm" another imbalances only yourself
- Nobody thinks they're the villain - everyone justifies their actions (even murderers claim "good causes")
- Love is a verb, not an emotion - your actions define whether you truly love

### Axiom 3: Repentance is True Justification
**Statement:** God's love is unconditional and God forgives everyone who repents.

**Sources:** Mark 1:15, Luke 5:31, Luke 15:7, Luke 24 (Great Commission)

**Key Points:**
- Jesus's great commission after resurrection: "Go and teach repentance for the forgiveness of sins"
- No mention of blood, no confession of beliefs required - just repentance
- Repentance (metanoia) = change of mind, change of thinking
- True repentance is not guilt - guilt maintains identification with sin
- Real repentance: philosophical insight that causes genuine transformation
- You see the error, become nauseated by it, and authentically want to change
- Guilt is the voice saying "I'm not forgiven" - anti-gospel
- Christianity's tools of guilt, shame, and condemnation are "anti-gospel tools from hell"
- Carl Jung's insight: Only alcoholics who had genuine spiritual experiences/repentance recovered

### Axiom 4: Judgment Binds, Forgiveness Liberates
**Statement:** Whatever you do to others in body or mind must be done unto you. Judgment keeps us bound; forgiveness sets us free.

**Sources:** Matthew 6:14, Matthew 7:1-2, Luke 6:37

**Key Points:**
- Perception is creation - how we perceive others affects consciousness
- When I hold someone prisoner in my mind, I imprison part of myself
- Judgment = casting someone out of your heart, seeing them as inferior
- Judgment is NOT discernment - noticing someone's condition to help them is not judgment
- Forgiveness is NOT approval of harmful actions or being a doormat
- Forgiveness = refusing to put anyone out of your heart
- The Law of One: Forgiveness is the only thing that can alleviate karma without experiencing it
- Kingdom of heaven is perfect peace - unforgiveness makes us a vibrational mismatch
- Forgiveness shows God we've learned the lesson

### Axiom 5: God Dwells Within You
**Statement:** The kingdom of heaven is within you. All beings are one with God.

**Sources:** Luke 17:21, John 14:20, John 10:30

**Key Points:**
- Jesus never taught separation from God
- "The kingdom of God is within you"
- Love the least of these because God dwells within them
- Ontological argument: If God is omnipotent, nothing can exist outside God's power
- Every atom belongs to God; all consciousness is sustained by God
- "In him we live and move and have our being" (Acts 17:28)
- You cannot be ontologically separate from God and still exist
- Billions of processes happening in your body every second - all God
- God is closer than you could possibly imagine

### Axiom 6: Righteousness Requires Self-Examination
**Statement:** We must constantly examine ourselves to see if our behaviors and thoughts measure up to the golden rule.

**Sources:** Matthew 7:3-5, Matthew 16:24, John 15:1-2

**Key Points:**
- No one else can do this self-auditing for you
- God puts the onus on each person to be self-responsible
- "Spiritual hygiene" - as important as physical hygiene
- Like walking through a swamp: regularly check yourself for "leeches" (distortions, sins)
- Self-examination is a self-love practice - you want to catch mistakes early
- When you see something wrong, thank God and celebrate the opportunity to improve
- The people we respect most are radically honest with themselves
- Examine yourself lovingly, not with guilt

### Axiom 7: Faith Without Works is Dead
**Statement:** Actions are what matter most to God. Belief without righteous action is meaningless.

**Sources:** Matthew 7:21-23, John 14:15, James 2:14-26

**Key Points:**
- Jesus turned away people who called him "Lord" but didn't do the works
- "If you love me, keep my commandments"
- James rebuked Paul: "Don't you know, oh vain man, that faith without works is dead?"
- Martin Luther acknowledged he couldn't square Paul and James on this
- The Jesus Way aligns with Yeshua and James over Paul
- Common saying: "Actions speak louder than words" - Christianity inverts this
- Your faith is good only insofar as it leads to righteous actions
- God knows what you believe by what you DO, not what you say

### Axiom 8: The Law of Reciprocity (Karma)
**Statement:** We reap what we sow. Whatever you do to others in body or mind must be done unto you.

**Sources:** Matthew 7:7, Galatians 6:7, Luke 6:38

**Key Points:**
- One of Jesus's most common teachings
- "Ask and it will be given; seek and you will find; knock and the door will be opened"
- Karma = your own doing, your own actions (Indian tradition)
- Karma in Law of One = inertia (movement in a certain direction)
- Negative karma creates inertia toward negative polarity
- Positive karma creates inertia toward the kingdom of heaven
- This law cannot be broken - it's the nature of the universe
- Faith = trusting in God's goodness even when we can't understand
- Worst things that happen can be most spiritually fruitful

### Axiom 9: The Body is a Temple
**Statement:** Create an environment of good health, obeying natural law, so your physical body can thrive.

**Sources:** 1 Corinthians 6:19-20, Matthew 6:22

**Key Points:**
- If you're "fat, sick, and nearly dead," loving your neighbor becomes much harder
- Your basic needs must be met before you can give to others (oxygen mask principle)
- Sickness burdens everyone around you; health benefits everyone
- No judgment on those who are sick - just a spiritual principle
- Health is of God; sickness is not of God
- Healthy living is silent worship and gratitude to the Creator
- Maximize your incarnation - the most spiritual progress often comes in later years
- A 100-year life could yield 3x more spiritual evolution than a 50-year life
- Health allows you to be a vessel for God's love longer

### Axiom 10: Service to Others
**Statement:** The positive polarity. How we polarize spiritually and demonstrate our love for God.

**Sources:** Matthew 25:40, Mark 10:45, John 13:14-15

**Key Points:**
- Left last not because least important, but because the previous 9 set the foundation
- Without repentance, forgiveness, self-examination, and health, your "service" can harm
- **Giving state:** Wherever you go, your first intention is "How can I help/bless this person?"
- **Receiving state:** Acceptance, surrender, allowing - never resist the present moment
- Master giving and receiving = master loving relationship
- Service doesn't require grand gestures - a smile, listening, attention are service
- Giving is proof of having - when you give love, you prove you ARE the source of love
- The selfish path is actually miserable; service is "the greatest gift we give ourselves"
- Our love for God can only be measured in how we love the least of these

## Misconceptions Addressed

### "Judgment is the same as discernment"
- Discernment is noticing reality to help someone (they're stuck, they need help)
- Judgment is a value judgment on who a being IS - casting them out of your heart
- You can't serve someone you won't accept where they are
- Loving someone stuck in addiction requires acknowledging their condition

### "Forgiveness means approving harmful actions"
- Forgiveness is NOT letting someone off the hook or being a doormat
- Forgiveness is refusing to put anyone out of your heart
- You can forgive someone AND maintain boundaries
- Forgiveness ends the cycle of harm; judgment perpetuates it

### "Guilt leads to repentance"
- Guilt maintains identification as the doer of sin - "I really am horrible"
- True repentance is philosophical insight: "That's not who I am, not my true nature"
- Guilt says "I'm not forgiven" - opposite of gospel
- Christianity's guilt/shame/condemnation approach is "anti-gospel tools from hell"
- Guilt actually keeps people stuck in addiction cycles

### "You can be ontologically separate from God"
- If God is omnipotent (all-powerful), nothing exists outside God
- "In him we live and move and have our being"
- Consciousness, body processes, atoms - all sustained by God
- Sending part of God's creation to eternal hell would mean God sending part of himself

### "Spirituality is about beliefs and experiences"
- Crystal necklaces, tarot, astral projection - meaningless without the Golden Rule
- Mystical experiences don't matter unless they make you more loving
- "I don't care what star system you're from" - can you love your neighbor?
- Actions speak louder than altered states
- The New Age has rampant "fake spirituality" - all talk, no embodiment

### "Service to others is a grand gesture"
- A smile, listening, attention - these are service
- Start with loving the grocery clerk, the rude driver
- Size of service doesn't matter to God - your HEART matters
- Get the basics right before jumping to Africa to dig wells

## Personal Reflections

This episode is a goldmine - essentially the "constitution" of the Jesus Way philosophy. The 10 axioms framework provides something I've been wanting: a simple, shareable summary of what this approach to Christianity actually teaches.

Most striking: the ruthless simplicity. Every axiom can be tested against the Golden Rule. Does blood atonement violate the Golden Rule? Yes (God requires violence). Reject it. Does eternal hell violate the Golden Rule? Yes (infinite punishment for finite "sins"). Reject it. This cuts through 2,000 years of accumulated theological complexity.

The distinction between guilt and repentance is crucial. Guilt = "I'm a bad person" (identification with sin). Repentance = "That's not who I am" (disidentification). Christianity weaponizes guilt; Jesus offered freedom. This reframes the entire project of spiritual growth.

The opening about Charlie Kirk's assassination contextualizes everything. These axioms aren't abstract philosophy - they're desperately needed medicine for a world that justifies violence because "I know I'm right." Pride as the root of all violence. Nobody thinks they're the villain.

The critique of New Age spirituality is refreshing. Aaron spent 10 years in those circles and names the problem: "fake spirituality," people using "conscious language" to bypass accountability, expensive retreats where gurus sleep with attendees. The Golden Rule cuts through all of it. Can you love your neighbor? Then everything else is "spiritual pampers pretending to be a tuxedo."
