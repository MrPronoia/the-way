## Probable Key Arguments

### 1. The Three Temples Framework

The human being is a threefold temple, corresponding to the structure of Solomon's Temple:

| Temple | Domain | Biblical Correspondence | Purification Method |
|--------|--------|------------------------|-------------------|
| **First Temple: The Body** | Physical health, diet, fasting, sexual purity | Outer Court -- where sacrifices (of appetite, comfort, vice) are made | The Jesus Diet, fasting, exercise, abstaining from flesh and intoxicants |
| **Second Temple: The Mind** | Thoughts, emotions, beliefs, shadow material | The Holy Place -- where the lampstand (illumination) and showbread (spiritual nourishment) reside | Shadow work (ep 41), prayer, meditation, discernment of thoughts vs. entity suggestions (ep 37) |
| **Third Temple: The Spirit** | Direct communion with God, Christ consciousness, kingdom awareness | The Holy of Holies -- where the Ark of the Covenant (God's presence) dwells | Surrender, contemplative prayer, practicing the presence of God (ep 8), selfless service |

**The sequential logic:** You cannot enter the Holy Place with an unpurified body. You cannot enter the Holy of Holies with an undisciplined mind. The temples are accessed in order. This is why the Jesus Diet and fasting come first -- not because physical health is the highest goal, but because it's the *gateway*.

### 2. Christ Consciousness as the Destination

Christ consciousness (defined in episode 17) is the awareness of the divine ideal of humanity alive within you. It's not a mystical superpower -- it's WWJD as a living, moment-to-moment practice:

- **Christ Vision** (episode 31): Seeing the divine in everyone, even enemies
- **Kingdom Consciousness** (episode 31): Awareness of God's perfect laws governing reality
- **The Teleological Man** (episode 17): The fully actualized human being, what God intended

The three temples framework provides the *path* to this state. Episode 17 described *what* Christ consciousness is. Episode 45 likely describes *how* to get there through the sequential purification of body → mind → spirit.

### 3. Why Most Christians Never Get There

The Pauline church teaches that Christ consciousness (if they used the term at all) is received through belief -- confess and believe, and you're saved. The Jesus Way argues this skips every step of the actual process:

- No bodily purification (most churches serve donuts and coffee)
- No mental discipline (no shadow work, no meditation tradition)
- No progressive sanctification through works
- The result: Christians who *believe* in Christ but don't *embody* Christ

James the Just embodied the three temples: vegetarian from birth (first temple), prayer so constant his knees were calloused (second temple), permitted to enter the Holy of Holies in the actual temple (third temple). He is the model.
