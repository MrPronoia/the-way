## Key Teachings

### The Spiritual Science of Sugar
- Sugar (glucose/fructose) is "crystallized sunlight" - pure prana captured through photosynthesis
- Glucose is the body's preferred and designed fuel source - the brain and nervous system run almost exclusively on it
- Fat/ketones are a backup system, not the body's preference
- Glucose is like gasoline; fat is like a coal engine (slower, more laborious metabolism)
- Fruit contains "light codes" that produce health and longevity benefits beyond mere nutrients

### Why Sugar Has Been Wrongly Demonized
- Sugar gets blamed for fat's problems when they're combined (donuts, ice cream, pizza)
- The cardinal sin of nutrition: mixing high fat with high carbohydrates
- Type 2 diabetes is caused by fat staying in the bloodstream too long, not sugar alone
- When glucose is eaten without fat, it doesn't cause the problems attributed to "sugar"
- Fruit teaches the body to use insulin properly again

### The mTOR/AMPK Balance
- **AMPK** (daytime): Longevity benefits, cellular cleaning, fasting state, high energy
- **mTOR** (evening): Rest and digest, muscle building, growth hormone
- Most people mixing macros never fully activate either pathway
- The Jesus Diet polarizes to each at the right times for maximum benefit
- Deep AMPK during the day slingshots into strong mTOR signaling at night

### Macronutrient Timing Protocol
- **Morning to mid-afternoon:** Fruit and fruit juice only (carbohydrate metabolism)
- **After workout (~3-4pm):** Protein shake
- **Evening meal:** Higher fat, higher protein
- Target macros: 50-85g protein, 30-50g fat, 400-500g carbohydrates daily
- This rhythm matches circadian biology

### Fructose and Longevity Hormones
- Fructose activates FGF-21 (fibroblast growth factor 21)
- Fructose activates GDF-15 (growth differentiation factor 15)
- Both increase metabolism and fat burning
- FGF-21 benefits: Improves insulin sensitivity, activates fatty acid oxidation, mimics fasting
- GDF-15 benefits: Strong appetite reduction, anti-inflammatory, linked to lifespan extension
- AMPK activation: Stimulates mitochondrial biogenesis, cellular cleaning

- Extremely muscle-sparing (carbs prevent gluconeogenesis)
- Reduces oxidative stress and reactive oxygen species (ROS)
- Creates euphoric, focused energy states
