## Summary

This solo episode presents Aaron Abke's core framework for understanding and embodying Christ consciousness, which he calls "The Art of Loving Relationship." He argues this is the single most important teaching he could offer - a first principle that summarizes Jesus's entire gospel message. The teaching provides a practical rubric for spiritual self-assessment: at any moment, you can ask whether you're in a state of "giving and receiving" (loving relationship) or "taking and keeping" (ego's inversion of relationship).

The episode begins with scholarly analysis of Philippians 2:5-8 ("Let the same mind be in you that was also in Christ Jesus"), demonstrating through form criticism that this passage is actually a pre-Pauline Nazarene hymn that Paul quoted. This establishes that striving to reach Christ consciousness was a core tenet of original Jewish Christianity - not a "new age" concept as modern Christianity often claims. Aaron then develops his framework showing that everything exists in relationship, oneness doesn't mean sameness but interconnection, and our state of being is determined by perception leading to thought leading to feeling.

The practical application centers on recognizing that the ego can only engage in "taking and keeping" (using and withholding), while Christ consciousness operates through "giving and receiving." These are opposites with no crossover, making it a clear litmus test for spiritual alignment. The key insight is that before you can take from or withhold from others, you've already been doing it to yourself - making radical self-awareness essential to the Jesus way.

## Key Teachings

- **Christ Vision/Christ Consciousness defined:** Experiencing Jesus's exact awareness of God and his love for people; seeing yourself in everyone else; letting "the same mind be in you that was also in Christ Jesus"
- **Everything exists in relationship:** Nothing in the universe exists in true isolation. This is the metaphysical foundation for oneness. God expresses through infinite unique forms, but all are interconnected
- **Oneness does not mean sameness:** Everything is unique (even identical twins), but nothing is truly separate. Oneness means everything exists in relationship
- **State of being formula:** Perception -> Thought -> Feeling. When you perceive contrary to truth/how God perceives, you feel negative emotions as spiritual feedback
- **Loving relationship = Giving and Receiving:** These are states of being (attitudes/dispositions), not just actions. They always go together - you can't truly give without receiving benefit, and what you receive is your gift to the giver
- **Ego's relationship = Taking and Keeping:** The inversion of loving relationship. Taking = using, attraction, attachment. Keeping = withholding, aversion, resistance. Both are negative/contracting functions
- **Lack does not exist:** There's only infinity in God's universe. "We only lack what we are not in relationship to" - lack is about free will choices of what to engage with
- **Essence vs. Substance:** To manifest desires, be in relationship with the essence (wealth/abundance mindset), not just the substance (money). Essence-substance mismatch blocks manifestation
- **Ego's three beliefs:** Lack -> Attachment -> Control. These generate suffering
- **Giving means:** Showing up with best attitude, effort, intention daily. Gratitude, openness, presence. "How can I help? Who can I bless?"
- **Receiving means:** Acceptance, surrender, non-resistance. Saying yes to life while still intervening with love when needed
- **Polarization:** Positive = radiating, outward, expansive (service to others). Negative = contracting, magnetic, inward (service to self). Not value judgments but functions
- **Core self-inquiry:** "In what ways do I take from myself?" and "In what ways do I withhold from myself?" - addressing the root of how we treat others
- **Ego as verb, not noun:** The ego is an activity in the mind, not an entity. It exists only as the function of identifying "I am" with forms. When the activity ceases, the ego is not present
- **The Christ = I Am:** The divine name, eternal conscious principle, first emanation of God. You are always first person present tense. Everyone shares this identical I Am feeling - this is how we are one
- **Enlightenment/Salvation defined:** The ability to be in loving relationship with everything at all times
- **Fourth density consciousness:** Heart chakra activation, living from love and selfless service. Most humans operate from third chakra (solar plexus/ego). The evolutionary leap is to the heart
- **Kingdom of Heaven:** Not a location but an awareness of God's perfect oneness and omnipresence. "Spread out across the earth, inside and outside you, but men are unaware of it" (Gospel of Thomas). Established through loving relationship
- **Everyone is a citizen of the Kingdom:** In God's mind, everyone belongs, whether they know it or not. If we can see it for them, we make it possible for them to see it

## Jewish Christianity Context

Aaron explicitly frames this teaching within early Jewish Christianity/Nazarene Christianity:

- **Striving for Christ consciousness was original:** Before Pauline theology and Greco-Roman paganization corrupted Christianity, the first Christians (the 12 disciples, the 72, the 5000+) strove to reach the same mind/consciousness as Jesus. This is now considered "heretical" and "new age" by modern Christianity but was a core tenet of the Jesus way
- **The Kenosis Hymn as Nazarene evidence:** The pre-Pauline hymn in Philippians 2 preserves early Nazarene theology because Paul spent time with the Nazarenes before being exiled as an apostate and false prophet. The hymn lacks Paul's typical themes (law/Torah, justification by faith, resurrection narrative) and uses vocabulary Paul doesn't use elsewhere
- **The Two Ways:** Identified as the oldest Christian philosophy on record, found in the Didache and Dead Sea Scrolls. Jesus and James both taught this framework. The way of life (positive polarity, service to others) vs. the way of death (negative polarity, service to self)
- **Paul's interpolation:** Scholars believe Paul added "even death on a cross" to the hymn to fit his crucifixion theology - the original hymn ended at "death" with perfect meter
