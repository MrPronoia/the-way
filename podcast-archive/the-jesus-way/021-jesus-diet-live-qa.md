## Key Teachings

### The Jesus Diet Protocol Recap
- **Morning to 3pm:** Fruit and fruit juice only (carbohydrate metabolism)
- **Mid-afternoon (~3pm):** Protein shake (banana, blueberries, honey, plant protein, creatine)
- **Evening:** One dinner meal with starch, vegetables, and plant protein (keep fat low)
- **Movement:** 10,000 steps daily minimum; strength training 3x/week

### Macronutrient Guidelines
- High carbohydrate, moderate protein, low fat diet
- Carbs: 250-500g daily (varies by activity level, gender, body size)
- Protein: 50-100g daily (less than commonly recommended)
- Fat: 30-60g daily (keep low to prevent body fat storage)
- Don't copy exact macros - experiment to find your optimal range

### Carbohydrate Intake Clarification
- Aaron gets 400-500g carbs from combination of sources, not just fruit
- Fruit/juice until 3pm: ~250-300g carbs
- Protein shake with banana, blueberries, honey: ~50g carbs
- Dinner with rice/noodles/potatoes: ~100g carbs
- Women may need less (around 250g); smaller men perhaps 300g
- "Try 250, then 300, then 350 - see where you feel best"

### Dinner Meal Structure
- One starch (rice, pasta, potatoes, quinoa)
- Vegetables (zucchini, broccoli, bell peppers, bok choy, mushrooms)
- Plant protein source (keep meal low fat overall)
- Can add 1-2 eggs if transitioning from animal-based
- Walk after dinner for digestion

### Recommended Plant Protein Sources
- **Seitan:** Upton Naturals brand - very high protein
- **Soy-free tofu:** Big Mountain brand (fava beans, water, sea salt) - 64g protein per package
- **Edamame pasta:** One Bean brand - ~40g protein per serving
- **Lentils:** "True protein superfood" - very nutrient-dense, easy to digest
- **Beans:** Chickpeas, black beans, white beans, kidney beans
- **Tempeh:** Fermented, easier to digest
- Combining protein sources (e.g., edamame pasta + seitan) provides complete nutrition

### Soy Debate Addressed
- Phytoestrogens in soy do NOT lower testosterone (backed by their blood work)
- Both hosts eat soy regularly with top-tier testosterone levels
- Some people digest soy better than others - experiment
- Soy actually helps optimize testosterone levels (bio-regulator effect)
- Aaron's estradiol was 15 (very low) while eating soy daily; testosterone near 800

### Blood Sugar Spikes - Debunked
- "Spiking blood sugar is NOT bad"
- "It's not about how high you fly, it's about how fast you fall"
- Quick descent after spike = healthy insulin sensitivity
- Problem is when blood sugar stays elevated (from combining fat with sugar)
- Fat in bloodstream prevents insulin from clearing glucose efficiently
- Type 2 diabetes caused by fatty acids lingering in bloodstream, not sugar alone

### Supplements Recommended
1. **Creatine:** Number one recommendation for everyone (meat eaters or not)
2. **Methylated B Complex:** B12/B6 depleted from modern soil; even factory cows need B12 injections
3. **Choline:** Especially if not eating eggs (4 eggs = daily choline requirement)
4. **Magnesium glycinate:** Good for sleep and overall health
5. **Organic pre-workout:** If needed for workouts
6. **Apigenin:** Great sleep supplement
7. Don't need as many supplements on this diet - the fruit provides tremendous nutrition

### On Eggs and Milk
- Only from local farms that treat animals with kindness - NO factory farms
- Eggs are "one of the healthiest foods biologists have ever studied"
- 4-6 eggs daily provides saturated fat, choline, protein
- Don't NEED eggs or milk to thrive, but they're convenient transition foods
- Chickens produce thousands of eggs; most will never be fertilized
- Strategic inclusion makes diet accessible to meat-eaters willing to transition

### Coffee and Caffeine
- Both hosts drink coffee, but with caveats
- Keep to one cup per day; no more than 300mg caffeine
- Source high-quality organic coffee (low-quality can be toxic)
- Have some fruit in stomach before coffee to reduce jitters
- You need less caffeine on this diet due to natural energy from fruit

### Exercise Requirements
- Walking is "the new running" - very muscle-sparing
- 10,000 steps daily minimum (Blue Zones longevity factor)
- 3+ days/week strength training (weights, yoga, plyometrics, kettlebells)
- Can't out-supplement lack of exercise
- "Movement sends your body a signal: I want to be alive"
- Sedentary lifestyle signals body for decline

### Fruit Fasting Protocol
- Eat nothing but fruit and fruit juice for 1-3 days
- Creates high metabolic state; rapidly burns body fat
- Muscle-sparing when glycogen stores are full
- Easier than water fasting
- Great to do after a cheat meal
- Helps break addictions (comes from lack of dopamine)

### Cheat Meals Philosophy
- Don't program them, but they happen (travel, birthdays, social events)
- When metabolism is primed from strict protocol, cheat meals can actually boost metabolism
- Often look leaner after cheat meal due to mTOR/IGF-1 signaling
- Can fruit fast the day after a big cheat to reset
- Don't have two cheat days in a row; get back on track quickly

### Adjustment Period
- Body needs time to develop enzymes for new foods
- Gas from beans normalizes after a few weeks
- "All great things take time to adjust to"
- Persevere through initial discomfort - "you'll feel better than you've ever felt"
- Switching to carnivore is far worse digestively than switching to plant-based

### On Plant Defense Chemicals
- The Jesus Diet emphasizes fruit, not leafy greens with defense chemicals
- "We don't eat plants, we eat what plants produce"
- Pick the tomato, the vine keeps producing
- Defense chemical concerns are overblown by carnivore advocates

### Dealing with Family/Social Pressure
- Common for vegetarians/vegans to be mocked
- Others project their own guilt onto you
- "Everyone knows deep down that killing innocent animals is not moral"
- Lead with love and understanding; don't get frustrated
- Your health and energy will convert people better than arguments
- Be patient - it's tested both hosts many times

"The answer is I don't get that many carbs from just fruit." Combination of fruit/juice (250-300g), protein shake ingredients (50g), and dinner starch (100g).

### Can I flip the macros and start with fat/protein in the morning?
No - the protocol specifically requires carbs-only until mid-afternoon to maximize AMPK signaling and longevity benefits.

### What about people who failed on vegan/vegetarian diets?
"Just because somebody doesn't know how to eat a vegan or vegetarian diet properly doesn't mean the diet can't be eaten properly." Diet requires research and understanding; most fail due to ignorance, not the diet itself.

### Do I need locally grown, organic fruit?
Ideal but not required. "Don't not do the diet because you can't find fresh locally grown produce." Do the best you can with what's available.

### Is oatmeal okay in the morning?
"Preferably no. We don't want starches in the morning because starches are not fast digesting sugar sources." Stick to fruit and fruit juice.

### What do you feed your dog?
James feeds his dog what he eats (stir fries, eggs). Their friend Cam has a 12-year-old dog that's been vegetarian its whole life and is extremely healthy and energetic.

### What about choline if not eating eggs?
Supplement with a choline supplement - they recommend taking one daily for brain health.
