## Summary

This episode presents a comprehensive framework for mystical prayer as taught in the Jesus Way tradition. Aaron and James introduce three distinct forms of prayer: Contemplative Prayer (conscious attunement of the soul to God), Active Prayer (affirmative, present-tense declaration aligned with God's nature), and Passive Prayer (practicing the presence of God throughout daily life). They emphasize that true prayer is not begging God for things or informing an omniscient deity of problems, but rather aligning oneself with God's already-perfect nature.

The hosts ground their prayer teaching in the "Five O's" of God's ontology from Episode 01: God is One, Omnipotent, Omniscient, Omnipresent, and Omnibenevolent. They argue that these immutable divine attributes should shape how we pray - since God already knows everything and has all power, prayer becomes about elevating our consciousness to God's level rather than trying to bring God down to our problems.

A significant portion of the episode covers practical application, including a healing testimony where group prayer for James's injured dog Nolly resulted in dramatic overnight recovery. The episode concludes with extensive quotes from 17th-century Christian mystic Brother Lawrence and his classic "Practicing the Presence of God," emphasizing that the ultimate goal is ceaseless awareness of divine presence in daily life.

## Key Teachings

### The Three Forms of Prayer

**1. Contemplative Prayer (Tefilah Levina)**
- Hebrew term meaning "prayer of insight"
- Definition: The conscious attunement of the soul to God
- Practice: Hold attention on divine qualities without intellectual analysis
- Let insights emerge through feeling rather than thinking
- Use the Five O's as anchor points for meditation
- Goal: Revelation (direct experience) rather than conceptual knowledge

**2. Active/Affirmative Prayer**
- Definition: Coming into alignment with God's will through first-person, present-tense declaration
- Based on Mark 11:24: "Whatever you ask in prayer, believe you've already received it"
- Combines clear intention with elevated emotion (heart-mind coherence)
- Declare what IS true of God's nature, not what you lack
- Example: "I AM free. I AM at peace. I AM abundant."

**3. Passive Prayer (Practicing the Presence)**
- Definition: Charging up our "God battery" through moment-to-moment awareness
- Coined by Brother Lawrence (17th century Christian mystic)
- Practice: 10-12 brief pauses daily (even 30 seconds) to remember God's presence
- Goal: Eventually ceaseless awareness of divine presence
- The presence eventually pursues you rather than you pursuing it

### The Five O's Applied to Prayer

| Attribute | Prayer Application | Meditation Focus |
|-----------|-------------------|------------------|
| Oneness | Forgiveness, healing through non-separation | Contemplate I AM; one great energy sustaining all |
| Omnipotence | Surrender, releasing fear and control | Meditate on infinity, natural law, light |
| Omniscience | Alignment with divine mind | Universe as inside God's mind; all answers available now |
| Omnipresence | Abiding in presence even when feeling distant | Eternity; God as animating power in body |
| Omnibenevolence | Giving and receiving love | Beauty of nature; unconditional love |

### Why Traditional Prayer Fails

- Praying from lack/separation perpetuates ego consciousness
- Begging God implies God doesn't know or care (violates omniscience/omnibenevolence)
- Trying to "pay" God something (blood sacrifice, suffering) ignores omnipotence
- Fear-based prayer connects to an imagined God, not the real one
- Must elevate consciousness to where God IS rather than asking God to come down

### The Elevator Analogy

- Seven-story building: Floor 1 = hell, Floor 7 = heaven
- You cannot pull Floor 7 down to Floor 3
- Heaven doesn't exist in your lack beliefs, fears, judgments
- You must ride the elevator (awareness) UP to where God already is
- Prayer is rising in consciousness, not begging from below

## Practical Applications

### Daily Prayer Structure
1. **Morning:** 5-O Contemplative Meditation (2 minutes each attribute, 10+ minutes total)
2. **Throughout Day:** 10-12 brief pauses to practice presence (30 seconds to 1 minute)
3. **When Challenges Arise:** Active/affirmative prayer declaring God's nature into the situation
4. **Evening:** Reflection, confession of any out-of-alignment thoughts

### Healing Testimony: Nolly the Dog
- James's dachshund slipped disc, couldn't move for 3 days, constant pain/whimpering
- Group gathered, did affirmative prayer visualizing healthy dog with gratitude
- "We felt it. We knew it." - Combined intention with elevated emotion
- Next morning: dog jumping around, went on 45-minute walk
- Example of collective prayer's exponential power (Law of Doubling)

- Set background reminder: "Practice the Presence"
- Every time you check phone, pause and connect with God
- Builds new neural pathway through repetition
- Cornfield analogy: walking new path until it becomes effortless

- Clears the vessel, making it easier to drop into prayer states
- Prayers during fasting described as "exponentially more profound"
- Monastic traditions (Mount Athos, Desert Fathers) combined fasting with contemplation
