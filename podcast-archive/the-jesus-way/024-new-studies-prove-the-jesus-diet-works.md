## Key Teachings

### The Protein Myth Debunked
- **62 combined studies** show the trend between protein intake and muscle growth is essentially flat
- Participants eating 82g/day gained the same muscle as those eating 164g/day
- Those eating 270g/day actually gained LESS muscle than lower-protein groups
- Dr. Eric Trexler (Duke University): "Many researchers aren't even confident that a benefit [from higher protein] exists at all"

### What Actually Builds Muscle
1. **Hard training** - the primary driver
2. **Eating enough calories** to fuel training and recovery
3. **Protein** - comes in third, as support (unless severely restricted)

### The Thermic Effect Problem
- Protein has a 25-30% thermic effect (uses that much of its calories just for digestion)
- Fat: 0-3% thermic effect
- Carbs: 5-10% thermic effect
- High protein diets waste enormous energy on digestion, reducing energy for youthfulness and cellular regeneration

### Longevity Hormones
- High protein consumption BLUNTS longevity hormones: AMPK, FGF-21, GDF-15
- These hormones slow biological aging, improve cellular turnover, increase energy, burn body fat while preserving muscle

### The Jesus Diet Protocol
- **60% carbs, 20% protein, 20% fat** (general guideline)
- Blue Zone populations eat even lower: **9-13% of calories from protein**
- Focus on bioavailability over nutrient density
- Fruit provides amino acids the body uses to synthesize its own protein

### Kidney Health Connection
- Animal protein is the absolute worst thing for kidney health
- James's personal experience: mother's kidney disease worsened by animal protein
- Kidney disease patients who give up animal protein have greatly reduced odds of dialysis
- Old Testament references to kidney as a spiritual organ for discerning truth
